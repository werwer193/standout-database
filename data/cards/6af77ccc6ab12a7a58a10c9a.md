## Barrage

**Throw a slower flurry of heavy punches, then knock the target back. The final punch breaks guard and can be perfect blocked.**

**Strength and Ability contribute equally to each punch.**

**Each blocked hit shows the defender’s remaining guard at that impact point.**

![flurry.gif](https://trello.com/1/cards/6af77ccc6ab12a7a58a10c9a/attachments/6b035f00a8c0ac1b5317e456/previews/6b035f06a8c0ac1b5317ed0a/download/flurry.gif)

**Base cooldown 23s**

## Erased Limbs

**Erasure, Erasure Leap, Bring to Me, Rib Breaker’s grab and the first hit of Erasure Combo each have a 10% chance to take an arm and a separate 5% chance to take a leg. These rolls also apply to a blocked palm hit. Whichever is closer to the palm takes the injury, the enemy or their exposed Stand.**

![image.webp](https://trello.com/1/cards/6af77ccc6ab12a7a58a10c9a/attachments/6b036224a2f5e4ff1602b71c/previews/6b036224a2f5e4ff1602b7d2/download/image.webp)

## Erasure

**Suck nearby enemies into your erasure, then swipe at them with The Hand’s right palm.**

**Damage scales equally with Strength and Ability.**

**Erasure deals 20–42 damage, breaks guard, can be parried, and stuns for 0.75 seconds. Erasure can also destroy terrain and walls.**

**Base cooldown 22s**

# Projectile Erasure

**Erase incoming projectiles in front of The Hand.**

## Rib Breaker

**Grab one enemy through their guard and drive a knee into their ribs.**

**A perfect block parries the grab. If The Hand misses, it pulls its arm back. On a catch, the grab deals 4–10 damage and the knee deals 6–14. The knee leaves the enemy stunned for 1.4 seconds, long enough to follow with a light attack.**

**Base cooldown 20s**

![rib.gif](https://trello.com/1/cards/6af77ccc6ab12a7a58a10c9a/attachments/6b035fae96631b4cf5b3e1c0/previews/6b035fb396631b4cf5b3e789/download/rib.gif)

## Erasure Leap

**Erase the space ahead of you to dash toward your cursor, taking nearby characters with you.**

**Keep aiming until The Hand takes off. It reaches 4.5 studs toward your cursor, then carries you up to 47.04 studs at 1 Ability or 141.12 studs at 166 Ability. Nearby characters within 12.6 studs can join you along the way. The palm strikes in front of you for 10–24 damage when you arrive. A frontal block stops the hit. While piloting, The Hand makes the leap within its pilot range.**

**Base cooldown 12s**

![erasureleap.gif](https://trello.com/1/cards/6af77ccc6ab12a7a58a10c9a/attachments/6b036011b486f46a03d330e0/previews/6b036014b486f46a03d33544/download/erasureleap.gif)

# EX [Awakening Moves]

## Erasure Combo (2 Bars)

**Swipe with your right palm, then follow with two punches. The last punch sends the enemy flying.**

**The swipe and first punch keep a clean hit going into the next strike. The swipe deals 12–28 damage. Each punch deals 8–16. The last punch knocks the enemy back for 0.25 seconds and leaves them ragdolled for another 1.2 seconds. Only the opening swipe can erase a limb.**

**Base cooldown 28s**

![combo.gif](https://trello.com/1/cards/6af77ccc6ab12a7a58a10c9a/attachments/6b03612513f67104bbceab4a/previews/6b03612813f67104bbceb36c/download/combo.gif)

## Bring to Me (1 Bar)

**Erase the space between you and players in front of you to pull them into punching range.**

**Strength and Ability contribute equally to pull and wall-collision damage. Aim up to 67.2 studs away at 1 Ability or 87.36 studs at 166 Ability. Marked enemies move 20% slower and are pulled to a point four studs in front of you. The pull deals 4–12 damage and stuns for 0.3 seconds when it lands. A downed enemy can be pulled while still on the ground.**

**Dragging someone through a breakable wall or prop deals collision damage once. With an empty hand, you can also pull in one loose throwable and catch it.**

**Base cooldown 20s**

![pull2.gif](https://trello.com/1/cards/6af77ccc6ab12a7a58a10c9a/attachments/6b03615a250011acdfb87f8b/previews/6b03615e250011acdfb885af/download/pull2.gif)

## Escape the pull

**The mark travels with the pull. Move more than 6.6 studs from its center on any axis to break free and regain your normal speed. Anyone still inside is pulled toward The Hand when the palm swings down.**
