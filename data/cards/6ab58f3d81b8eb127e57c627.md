## Original description

## Lore:

**“This stone moves with no regard to my will.”**

**The Stand of Scolippi. An automatic stone that follows people destined to die, changing its shape to reveal their approaching fate. Its touch offers a peaceful death. Even if its user cannot command it, and breaking the stone does not erase the fate it has foretold.**

## Stone Deity

**Use E to release Rolling Stones. It follows its own targets and cannot be recalled.**

![summon.gif](https://trello.com/1/cards/6ab58f3d81b8eb127e57c627/attachments/6ab656288cab13f835806051/previews/6ab656298cab13f835806235/download/summon.gif)

## Spiritual Connection

**use Ctrl to watch from the stone. This only changes your view. If you die, the stone remains, removes its current target, and moves to the next.**

## We Are All Slaves Of Fate

**Eight seconds after summon, a random player or NPC becomes Roling Stone’s target and receives a hidden timer of 120 seconds. The user cannot be the first target, Rolling and leaping begin after the first 10% of the timer; until then the stone can still melt and reappear near its target. Its rolling speed rises as that person’s timer runs down. If the target evades touching the stand for the entire timer, the stone then chooses someone else. Rolling Stones leaps over obstacles or melts and reappears when it cannot clear the route.**

## Ruthlessness Is Mercy

**If the stone touches its current target, that target dies immediately and painlessly. Contact with anyone else is harmless.**

![kill.gif](https://trello.com/1/cards/6ab58f3d81b8eb127e57c627/attachments/6ab65ac30d86eef350cd7327/previews/6ab65ac60d86eef350cd759f/download/kill.gif)

## Forbidden Throwable

**Pick up the stone with the normal throwable controls.**

![image.webp](https://trello.com/1/cards/6ab58f3d81b8eb127e57c627/attachments/6ab65a6e54b4014461e24239/previews/6ab65a6e54b4014461e242c8/download/image.webp)

## It’s Not My Time Yet

**While someone else is the stone’s current target, damage to someone holding the stone is absorbed by the stone instead. Carrying the stone prevents the holder from attacking or healing, and moving too far away from the stone’s target will cause it to melt through their hands to escape.**

## Within Death’s Reach

**The stone rolls after its target and leaps when close.**

## Unearthed Insight

**The stone changes form as it loses health.**

## Steintossen

**While holding Rolling Stones as a throwable, launch it in a stronger aimed arc. The impact damages and knocks back nearby enemies, breaking fragile scenery.**

**Base cooldown: 10s**

![TOSS.gif](https://trello.com/1/cards/6ab58f3d81b8eb127e57c627/attachments/6ab6595c075b11d5304ea409/previews/6ab65962075b11d5304eb197/download/TOSS.gif)

## **Sculpted Destruction**

**Strike nearby Rolling Stones with a sledgehammer, sending stone fragments forward. Each strike advances the stone one health stage: regular to damaged, then destroyed.**

**Base cooldown: 16s**

![BREAK.gif](https://trello.com/1/cards/6ab58f3d81b8eb127e57c627/attachments/6ab6597732869ed7283e8d3f/previews/6ab6597932869ed7283e8da6/download/BREAK.gif)

## Written in Stone

**Carve a short message into the top of nearby Rolling Stones. Enter up to 48 characters, then confirm. Taking damage interrupts carving. A new inscription replaces the old one.**

**Base cooldown: 8s**

![fart.gif](https://trello.com/1/cards/6ab58f3d81b8eb127e57c627/attachments/6ab65a1e3bafbbeda97fafc2/previews/6ab65a223bafbbeda97fb33b/download/fart.gif)

## Super Glue

**Hold beside Rolling Stones to kneel and repair 5% of its maximum health every half second, for up to 10 seconds. Can rebuild rubble. Release to stop; damageto you or the stone interrupts the repair.**

# EX [Awakening Moves]

## Prolong The Inevitable [3 Bars]

**Reset the current target’s hidden timer to its original duration.**

**Base cooldown: 25s**

![prolong.gif](https://trello.com/1/cards/6ab58f3d81b8eb127e57c627/attachments/6ab65c705487914b50bfa74d/previews/6ab65c725487914b50bfaaf8/download/prolong.gif)

**(Still locked onto the target, just resetting its timer.)**

## Passing Time [3 Bars]

**Remove half the current target’s starting time from their remaining timer, bringing their escape closer.**

**Base cooldown: 20s**

![gofast.gif](https://trello.com/1/cards/6ab58f3d81b8eb127e57c627/attachments/6ab65d4b4e5ece51f143cc51/previews/6ab65d4f4e5ece51f143d6f4/download/gofast.gif)

**(Cutting the timer in half, Making it scale its speed up.)**

## Changing Fate [3 Bars]

**Replace the stone’s current target with the nearest eligible person to you, starting a fresh hidden timer.**

**Base cooldown: 30s**

![change.gif](https://trello.com/1/cards/6ab58f3d81b8eb127e57c627/attachments/6ab65da8f9a653e5548a9fb9/previews/6ab65daaf9a653e5548aa599/download/change.gif)

**(Changing its target while going towards farther dummy, Resetting its timer and going for the closer one from square one.)**

## Fated Calm [3 Bars]

**Pause Rolling Stones and its hidden timer. It stops hunting and choosing targets until someone picks it up.**

**Base cooldown: 20s**

![stop.gif](https://trello.com/1/cards/6ab58f3d81b8eb127e57c627/attachments/6ab65cfaa095e4c4206c6aa6/previews/6ab65cfba095e4c4206c6ba2/download/stop.gif)

**(Completely stopped in its tracks, Resuming only when picked up.)**
