## **Lore:**

## **“Welcome… to the “True Man’s World.”**

**The Stand of Ringo Roadagain. By turning the dial on his wristwatch, Ringo can rewind time by exactly six seconds. He uses this ability to correct his errors and achieve perfection in a duel.**

## Watch Set

**Start a countdown that automatically rewinds you and nearby targets.**

**Once the watch is set, you can move and fight while the countdown runs. The rewind happens automatically when time runs out.**

**Base cooldown: 70s**

![rewindwatch.gif](https://trello.com/1/cards/6ab43549a0e096062fb7b5aa/attachments/6ab532bc372b5130944e8a9e/previews/6ab532d2372b5130944ea9bc/download/rewindwatch.gif)

## EX Moveset

# TRUE MAN’S WORLD (5 Bars)

**Spend a full EX gauge to rewind after a lethal hit during the next 30 seconds. Use while bleeding out to rewind immediately. Nearby opponents rewind with you.**

**Base cooldown: 90s**

## Point Blank (2 Bars)

**Grab an opponent through guard and strike them at point-blank range, leaving them in reach for a follow-up. A perfect block stops the grab.**

**ABILITY SCALING: 8 base damage, up to 18 at 166 Strength, weighted 80% Strength and 20% Ability.**

**Base cooldown: 22s**

![mandom.gif](https://trello.com/1/cards/6af25325efcf475a0337cdc9/attachments/6b01a1707337e134e157efb1/previews/6b01a1747337e134e157f5a7/download/mandom.gif)

## Shovel Slam (1 Bar)

**Slam a shovel down to break guard and send opponents tumbling forward. A perfect block stops the hit.**

**ABILITY SCALING: 10 base damage, up to 22 at 166 Strength, weighted 80% Strength and 20% Ability.**

**Base cooldown: 18s**
