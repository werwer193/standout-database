## Original description

# Every Stand barrage is a evasive that can be used in stun at the cost of 1 EX bar.

# If you are using a Stand without a barrage or built in evasive your subability EX move is a evasive.

# If you are using a Stand with a barrage evasive or built in evasive your subability EX move is a regular attack.
