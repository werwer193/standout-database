## Lore:

**“MREOOOWWWW”**

**The Stand of Wild Cat Size, the three cats encountered on Hawaii island, It turns their fur into nearly invisible strings with the strength of steel. Once a strand catches a limb, it ties itself tight and digs deeper as it constricts. Working together, the cats can weave their strings into a net and surround prey before it realizes it has been caught.**

## **Cat Pounce**

**Brace briefly, then pounce forward and knock back the first enemy you catch.**

**Base cooldown: 25s**

## String Lash

**Lash strings in front of you, repeatedly cutting enemies and knocking aside incoming projecitles.**

![two.gif](https://trello.com/1/cards/6ab4353318a53d55628fe2f7/attachments/6ab440c851cade06396e24fa/previews/6ab440e351cade06396e4160/download/two.gif)

## String Snare

**Leave a string loop on the ground. The first enemy to step into it is pulled into the air by their ankle. Heavy hits knock them free. Placing another replaces your old trap.**

**Base cooldown: 18s**
