## Original description

## **Lore:**

**“When we truly think “I’ll kill you!” from the bottom of our hearts… by that time, the deed has already been done!”**

**The Stand of Prosciutto, an assassin from La Squadra Esecuzioni. It rapidly ages living things for it. The warmer a victim’s body, the faster they weaken; keeping cold can slow the effect.**

## Ungraceful Aging

**Aging affects allies and enemies. Your neck and shoulders droop as stacks rise, and your attacks and dashes steadily weaken. Near 100 stacks, attack speed is reduced by up to 60% and dash power by up to 80%. At 100 stacks, players die immediately; PvE mobs cannot be killed by the stack cap. Each punch adds two stacks; Withering Mist adds two stacks every five seconds. After six seconds without more Aging, lose one stack every two seconds. Cleansing or death clears Aging.**

![m1sgratef.gif](https://trello.com/1/cards/6ab635789e86f9b20d8782e3/attachments/6ab63f32fd3e4fcfb6139e60/previews/6ab63f35fd3e4fcfb613a10a/download/m1sgratef.gif)

## Withering Mist [TOGGLE]

**Surround Grateful Dead with aging mist, Everyone nearby except you gains Aging and takes a little damage, including allies. Use again to stop**

**Base cooldown: 1.5s**

![withering.gif](https://trello.com/1/cards/6ab635789e86f9b20d8782e3/attachments/6ab63fa0512c70130ae029fa/previews/6ab63fa7512c70130ae041e6/download/withering.gif)

## Rotten Grasp

**When piloting, Grateful Dead holds an enemy for 4 seconds, applies up to 8 Aging stacks, then throws them left. You can still turn or leave pilot mode while it holds them. Otherwise, you grab them yourself for 2 seconds, apply up to 16 stacks, and drop them. Use again during either hold to release early.**

**Base cooldown: 12s**

## One-Armed Barrage

**Balance on one arm and pummel anyone in front of you with the other. You can turn, but cannot move during the barrage. Every third hit on the same target adds 1 Aging stack.**

**Base cooldown: 23s**

![bara.gif](https://trello.com/1/cards/6ab635789e86f9b20d8782e3/attachments/6ab640ef76a94c2104945539/previews/6ab640f476a94c2104945a0a/download/bara.gif)

## Senior Citizen [TOGGLE]

**Age yourself to gain EX over time. Your attacks become 60% slower and your dashes lose 80% of their power. Use again to return to normal. This form cannot kill you; Aging from enemies still builds up seperately**

**Base cooldown: 1s**

![senior.gif](https://trello.com/1/cards/6ab635789e86f9b20d8782e3/attachments/6ab6416c8e5f18e0e011d848/previews/6ab6416d8e5f18e0e011d8bd/download/senior.gif)

# EX [Awakening Moves]

## On The Train [3 Bars]

**Spread Withering Mist much farther for 20 seconds. Activates the mist if not already running.**

**Base cooldown: 30s**

![mistt.gif](https://trello.com/1/cards/6ab635789e86f9b20d8782e3/attachments/6ab6422abc791696d4e8e625/previews/6ab6422fbc791696d4e8ea30/download/mistt.gif)

## **Ice Pack [3 Bars]**

**Toss a bag of ice for someone else. Touching it clears Aging and protects them from Aging and Withering Mist for 30 seconds. Ragdolling knocks it off. Unclaimed bags disappear after 5 seconds.**

**Base cooldown: 40s**

![ice.gif](https://trello.com/1/cards/6ab635789e86f9b20d8782e3/attachments/6ab642501fd32be2a989a6c2/previews/6ab642541fd32be2a989abf1/download/ice.gif)
