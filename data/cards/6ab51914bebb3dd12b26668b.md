## Original description

**Vampires were created by the Pillar Men with acupuncture techniques that they developed through the use of the Stone Mask, which was created by Kars.**

**Vampires are known for their numerous superhuman traits. The most obvious abilities are their raw strength, regeneration, blood-sucking tentacles at their fingers and near-immortality. On top of that, many vampires are capable of manipulating their bodies in creative and unique ways.**
**It is to note that hamon is the most effective against vampires because it is channeling sunlight into their opponent. Meaning in-game vampires take more damage from hamon charged attacked.**
