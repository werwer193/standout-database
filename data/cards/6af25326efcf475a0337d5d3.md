# Lore:

## **“My skin’s been coarse and sandy ever since I was born.”**

**Charming Man’s Stand lets him break his skin into fragments, blend into his surroundings and disguise his appearance. He can also detach body parts and control them from a distance.**

## Chameleon

**Crouch near walls at any time to gain stealth. Uncrouching or attacking reveals you.**

![chameleon.gif](https://trello.com/1/cards/6af25326efcf475a0337d5d3/attachments/6af446eb9967360c8109824d/previews/6af446ed9967360c810986fe/download/chameleon.gif)

## Hand-Eye Coordination

**Tap Ctrl to detach your dagger hand, or hold it to detach an eye. The hand has short range but can use basic attacks and dagger skills. The eye can be used to scout at a much larger range. Press ctrl again or take a hit to stop controlling them. Using M1 while piloting the eye marks enemies in sight of the eye for you and your party members for 8 seconds. This has a 16 second cooldown.**

## Blend In

**Hide for up to six seconds. Attacking reveals you and gives your next hit 50% more damage. Taking damage or pressing again also ends it. Enemies can still hit you and hear your footsteps.**

**Base cooldown: 20s**

![blend.gif](https://trello.com/1/cards/6af25326efcf475a0337d5d3/attachments/6af44765bd128ee8646b73ed/previews/6af44767bd128ee8646b75a3/download/blend.gif)

## Shank Barrage

**Slash repeatedly in front of you. Then finish with a heavier slice that knocks targets away and causes Bleed for four seconds. Can be blocked or perfect blocked.**

**Base cooldown: 14s**

![barragesss.gif](https://trello.com/1/cards/6af25326efcf475a0337d5d3/attachments/6af447fb7f23c2be5610f70e/previews/6af448047f23c2be5610fbfc/download/barragesss.gif)

## Fragmented

**Avoid the next direct hit for 1.25 seconds, leaving a copy behind. You become hidden, invulnerable and much faster for one second. Press again to end the movement early, or attack to return with 50% more damage on your next hit.**

**Base cooldown: 20s**

![countr.gif](https://trello.com/1/cards/6af25326efcf475a0337d5d3/attachments/6af448a981b1d75082a30e71/previews/6af448ab81b1d75082a310fd/download/countr.gif)

## Go For The Throat

**Lunge forward with your dagger. A hit causes Bleed and stops the enemy from speaking for six seconds. Breaks guard instead if they are blocking. Can be perfect blocked.**

**Base cooldown: 16s**

![throat.gif](https://trello.com/1/cards/6af25326efcf475a0337d5d3/attachments/6af4492908334691b4f3f214/previews/6af4492e08334691b4f3f5ff/download/throat.gif)

## EX Moves [Awakening Moves]

## Eye Slash [1 Bar]

**Cut across nearby enemies, blinding them for 3.5 seconds. Can be blocked or perfect blocked.**

**Base cooldown: 18s**

![eyess.gif](https://trello.com/1/cards/6af25326efcf475a0337d5d3/attachments/6af4497fef62e9c6b341615b/previews/6af44980ef62e9c6b341623c/download/eyess.gif)

## Identity Theft [1 Bar]

**Steal the nearest person’s ID in front of you without breaking camouflage, Hold to open your wallet and copy a stolen name and appearance from your collected IDs. Attacking or taking damage ends the disguise; hold to change back. Keep up to three IDs until you die.**

**Base cooldown: 12s**

![IDD.gif](https://trello.com/1/cards/6af25326efcf475a0337d5d3/attachments/6af44ac25771ff80412b0440/previews/6af44ac45771ff80412b0727/download/IDD.gif)

## Held:

![wallet.gif](https://trello.com/1/cards/6af25326efcf475a0337d5d3/attachments/6af44af234a16bd9d9725cf1/previews/6af44af934a16bd9d97268cc/download/wallet.gif)

## False Evidence [1 Bar]

**Disguise yourself as a bush, box or trashcan for as long as you like. You move slowly and cannot sprint. Hold to choose the prop, tap to change into it. Attacking ends the disguise and gives your next hit 50% more damage. Taking damage, dashing or using other skills also ends it.**

**Base cooldown: 15s**

![box.gif](https://trello.com/1/cards/6af25326efcf475a0337d5d3/attachments/6af44b84f42b2302fe506ec8/previews/6af44b87f42b2302fe507483/download/box.gif)

## Shank Impale [2 Bars]

**Grab an enemy and stab them three times, then pull out the blade and shove them away, causing Bleed for four seconds. Bypasses block and cannot be interrupted.**

**Base cooldown: 28s**

![stabb.gif](https://trello.com/1/cards/6af25326efcf475a0337d5d3/attachments/6af44bd2daa13c344d8a6ea6/previews/6af44bd6daa13c344d8a73d6/download/stabb.gif)
