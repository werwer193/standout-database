# Lore:

## “Courage is knowing fear, and making that fear your own!”

**Will Anthonio Zeppeli, known as Baron Zeppeli, is a Hamon master from Italy who has devoted his life to finding the Stone Mask and destroying it.**

## Sendo Wave Kick! (You get this move when you have a stand.)

**You dash forward kicking forward with your right leg. The kick stuns and slightly knockbacks the first target hit. If the kick is used at close range, the user will not dash. If used at point blank range, the kick will do higher knockback and ragdoll as well as more damage.**

**Ability Scaling: Ability increases the lingering Ripple damage, up to 50% more at 150 Ability.**

**Base cooldown: 12s**

![sendo.gif](https://trello.com/1/cards/6af25326efcf475a0337d315/attachments/6af3515dd2f29deb2c501590/previews/6af3515ed2f29deb2c5016b1/download/sendo.gif)

## Tornado Overdrive! (You get this move when you have a stand.)

**While on the ground/not in air, spring straight upwards. Press again while in the air to start a hamonic spin and begin flying in the direction you are aiming. You can steer the direction, but gravity will eventually pull you down. Enemies you hit while spinning are dragged along with you. The final hit knockbacks and ragdolls them away.**

**Ability Scaling: Ability increases the lingering Ripple damage, up to 50% more at 150 Ability.**

**Base cooldown: 15s**

## Sendo Hamon Overdrive

**Punch a wall infront of you, causing Hamon to burst out of the other side. Enemies hit by this burst take triple damage and are ragdolled, If an enemy is on the same side of the wall as you, a ripple will travel down the wall and into the ground and auto track the nearest target.**

**If used without a wall, you punch the ground instead. The ripple runs straight forward instead and carries enemies with it. Punching a water or juice puddle with this variant causes the ripple to track the nearest enemy as if you had punched a wall.**

**Ability Scaling: Ability increases the lingering Ripple damage, up to 50% more at 150 Ability.**

**Base cooldown: 16s**

## Puddles

**Punching a water or juice puddle with Sendo Hamon Overdrive causes the ripple to track to the nearest enemy as if you had punched a wall. The puddle is used up in the process.**

## Breathe

**Hold to breathe and fill your Hamon. the longer you hold, the more you gain. While you have any charge you heal over time and your punches Carry Hamon. Your attacks, including Stand attacks and abilities, deal 30% more damage to vampires. Getting hit stops the breathing but you keep what you built. Above half charge you can walk up walls.**

**Ability scaling: A full charge lasts 28 seconds at 1 Ability and 35 seconds at 150 Ability. Ability strengthens healing by up to 50%. The 30% damage bonus against vampires is fixed.**

**Base cooldown: 35s**

![breath.gif](https://trello.com/1/cards/6af25326efcf475a0337d315/attachments/6af35f50033b85d1e7ab88fc/previews/6af35f52033b85d1e7ab8c85/download/breath.gif)

# EX Moves (Awakening Moves)

## Ripple Cutter [2 Bars]

**You take a sip from your glass, then spit out 6 ripple blades. Without shift lock they fly straight ahead of you. With shift lock they fly at your cursor. Each hit procs bleeding for 4 seconds and applies Ripple. Blades that hit the ground or run out of range leave a puddle of juice which Sendo Hamon Overdrive can use.**

**Ability scaling: Ability strengthens the lingering Ripple damage applied by Hamon.**

**Base cooldown: 20s**

![cutter.gif](https://trello.com/1/cards/6af25326efcf475a0337d315/attachments/6af361553c502e8452c3d9e5/previews/6af361593c502e8452c3e037/download/cutter.gif)

## Life Magnetism Overdrive [1 Bar]

**This must be used while in the air. Leaves gather above your head into a glider using Hamon magnetism. You glide forward for up to 8 seconds. Taking damage breaks the glider.**

**Base cooldown: 25s**

![life.gif](https://trello.com/1/cards/6af25326efcf475a0337d315/attachments/6af361d8184d9b1fd765a52d/previews/6af361e3184d9b1fd765ae22/download/life.gif)

## Forced Hamon Breathing [3 Bars]

**You drive a fist into the stomach of whoever is in front of you. An ally is healed and breathes Hamon for 20 seconds. A vampire takes heavy Hamon damage and is stunned for longer.**

**Hold while you are below 25% health to use Deep Pass Overdrive. You give away the last of your Hamon, the one you hit getting half of their health back at once and breathes Hamon for 60 seconds granting Hamonic buffs as well as 25% more damage. You lose the ability to use Hamon moves, age to the max limit, and your health drains away until you die.**

**Base cooldown: 35s**

**Ability scaling: Detection range grows from 140 to 210 studs with Ability.**

**Base cooldown: 25s**
