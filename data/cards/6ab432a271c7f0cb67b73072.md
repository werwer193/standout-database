## Blood (Sustain)

**Your blood reserve appears above the skill list. Combat refills it and recovery spends it. Regenerate 20% faster with blood, or 20% slower without it. In PvP, recovery needs blood. Healing restrictions still apply.**

![image.webp](https://trello.com/1/cards/6ab432a271c7f0cb67b73072/attachments/6ac261ed7c2f5d4fa9674a71/previews/6ac261ed7c2f5d4fa9674afb/download/image.webp)

## Sunlight (Weakness)

**Direct sunlight burns you after half a second. Full shade lets the exposure fade. Substantial opaque cover can protect you; glass and ordinary lights do not change sunlight exposure. The Sun’s active heat zone can also expose you.**

## Ripple (Weakness)

**Hamon attacks apply Ripple for 3 seconds. Repeated hits build it up to a cap. Ripple burns vampires more strongly and stops passive regeneration until it fades or is cleansed.**

## Undead Body (Passive)

**Eating Rokaka removes your Vampire sub ability and applies its stone exchange. Vampires can also eat apples to remove their Stand and use their full Vampire moveset, but cannot eat other food or use healing consumables. Ordinary healing restores only a quarter as much health. Vampire stays active alongside your Stand. Your dash lasts 40% longer, briefly hides your body and leaves dark-red afterimages behind you. (You also get red eyes during the night)**

# Moveset:

## Blood Drain

**Rush forward and grab an enemy. Drain their blood to refill your blood meter and heal, then throw them to your left. You can turn while feeding.**

**Base cooldown: 18s**

![blood.gif](https://trello.com/1/cards/6ab432a271c7f0cb67b73072/attachments/6ae0c684483ea6dbbb69b236/previews/6ae0c686483ea6dbbb69b533/download/blood.gif)

## Space Stingy Ripper Eyes

**Move your aim to sweep the beams through enemies and breakable cover. They hit repeatedly and deal more damage on headshots. Stuns cannot stop the attack. Firing costs 2 health and briefly blurs your vision. You cannot use it while piloting your Stand.**

**Base cooldown: 12s**

![stingyripper.gif](https://trello.com/1/cards/6ab432a271c7f0cb67b73072/attachments/6ac2690d4d5a83232671cf16/previews/6ac2690f4d5a83232671d3be/download/stingyripper.gif)

## Vaporatization Freezing Technique

**Freeze an enemy solid for 3 seconds, stopping their attack. A heavy blow shatters the ice for 25 extra damage; a killing blow breaks them apart. Light hits leave the ice intact. Heat melts it.**

**Base cooldown: 18s**

![freeze.gif](https://trello.com/1/cards/6ab432a271c7f0cb67b73072/attachments/6ae0c6d28e8426c799afd928/previews/6ae0c6d48e8426c799afe46c/download/freeze.gif)
