## **Overview:**

**Within the Train Raid, you act as a rogue member of the gang Passione, travelling from cart to cart defeating enemies and uncovering clues.**

## **CART VARIATIONS:**

### **THESE RANDOMISE PER RAID.**

### **VARIANTS:**

**Help the ‘Old Lady’ find a certain belonging, this can be the following: Lemonade & her scarf. After returning the item to her, you must locate the pin in her purse to picklock the door to the next Cart.**

**Protect Coco jumbo for 140 seconds.**

**Clear 2 waves of enemies, some stand users, some not. - 5 NPC's per wave. (Depending on your party size)**

**Obtain an ice-pack to prevent yourself from aging, and then hide Coco Jumbo under a seat. Once this is complete you must protect the civilians from aging and/or dying from Prosciutto by giving them ice packs. (Protect them for 1:45)**

**Find the conductors key to open the next car. (Must dodge Pesci's line by unequipping your stand and crouching behind couches)**

**Find the conductors key to open the next car. (No threat)**

**Kill Prosciutto. (CART LOCKED, NOT RANDOMISED)**

**Fight 3 Passione members on the roof of the cart (The boss can have any pinnacle stand) while the others have a randomised, non pinnacle stand. After defeating them all you must break the roof of the cart to re-enter. (Obstacle's will appear and you have to dodge them)**

**The 3 hints quest. You must find 3 hints (This includes items on the ground.) then you must question the passengers (They will say stuff like "I don't wanna see you." or "I travel alone." After prompting they will reveal themselves as Passione members, you must kill them and then you can enter the next cart.**
