## Original description

## Lore:

**“I’m going to turn you into a pin-cushion!”**

**The Stand of Jean Pierre Polnareff. An armored, humanoid Stand that wields a rapier with masterful skill. It is highly agile and can shed its heavy silver armor to move at blinding speeds, creating afterimages to confuse opponents.**
