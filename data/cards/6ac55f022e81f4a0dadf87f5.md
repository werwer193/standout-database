## Lore:

**“Did you see it? The thorns from my hand? That is my Stand!”**

**The Stand of Joseph Joestar. Thorn-covered purple vines extend from his hands, letting him bind opponents, swing between buildings, and channel Hamon. Its spirit photography can reveal distant people and places through cameras and televisions, or trace a map in dust.**

## Hermit’s Sling / Swing [TAP] / Pull [HOLD]

**Tap to catch a high surface to either side and swing forward through open space, releasing upward. Successive swings favor alternating sides. Hold to pull toward your aim; release to let go. Press Jump for extra lift. No cooldown outside of combat.**

**Base cooldown: 5s**

## Boulder Hold / Pull Rock / Throw

**Brace and pull a rock from the ground. While holding it, use slower, heavier basic attacks that deal more damage and stun. Use again to spin with hyperarmor, repeatedly knocking nearby enemies away before throwing the rock. It bursts on impact to hit nearby targets.**

**Base cooldown: 24s**
