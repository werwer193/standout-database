## Original description

Added the following roka types

Emerald Roka - Chance to remove stand, grants EXP in return (tier scales chance to remove, and exp gained) (this is to go from pure stand, mixed stand, to pure subability)
Sapphire Roka - Chance to remove subability, grants EXP in return (tier scales chance to remove, and exp gained) (this is meant for going back to pure stand, or swapping subablilty)
Amethyst Roka - Works like a regular roka, but EX (and other bars, like hamon and vampire) bar instead of HP
Opal Roka - Chance to turn TW into TW:AU (will turn other stands into variants in the future, such as KQ into KQ:AU or SP into SP [P4]), Small chance to wipe your stand. Tier scales % chance to work, and chance of wiping, S tier opal roka has 0% chance to wipe
Rokas that remove stand/ability/transform stands are NOT able to be used in splash mode containers

mutated rokas are obtainable via a 3% chance (per fruit) when growing
