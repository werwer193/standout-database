## Original description

## **Lore:**

**“I’m overflowing with hope and motivation!”**

**The Stand of Sale, a member of Passione. Kraft Work fixes objects in place and stores force in them through repeated strikes. Sale uses it to stop bullets, pin opponents against surfaces and climb on objects suspended in midair. Releasing a charged object sends it flying.**

## Krafted

**Kraft Work’s heavy attacks, hits from projectiles you froze, Fixation and Kinetic Field leave enemies Krafted for 10 seconds. Contact Fixation and touching your frozen objects also mark enemies. A heavy attack that releases a Kinetized enemy does not add a new mark or refresh one. Hold Werk to turn nearby enemies with your mark Kineticized, or use Tapping with Malicious Intent to direct frozen objects at them.**

## Kinetic Control

**With Kraft Work summoned, block just before a projectile reaches its front to freeze it. While blocking, use R just before a projectile hits to deflect and freeze it.**

**You can freeze 15 objects at once. They unfreeze when you dismiss Kraft Work or they leave your range. Punch frozen objects with M1 to charge them up to 6 times and change their direction. At full charge they fly three times as fast and deal up to 120% more impact damage. Use R to launch an object, or tap Werk to release everything. Shots that can bounce get one extra bounce.**

## Fixation

**Freeze projectiles, loose throwables, vehicles and piloted Stands around Kraft Work, and mark enemies Krafted.**

**Enemies caught in the burst become Krafted for 10 seconds. The burst freezes projectiles from any direction and loose throwables, and stops vehicles, trains and piloted Stands for 4 seconds. Tap Werk to release them early. More ability makes the burst larger.**

**Base cooldown: 12s**

## Contact Fixation

**Touch an enemy with Kraft Work to leave them Kineticized, or freeze a loose throwable to charge and launch.**

**Kineticized lasts 4 seconds. Light attacks build up to 6 charges and change the enemy’s launch direction. Charged enemies launch them when the hold ends. Use R or Werk to throw them early. The touch can be blocked. Touching an unheld throwable freezes it like your other frozen objects; punch it to charge and aim it, then launch it.**

**Base cooldown: 16s**

## Werk

**Tap to release your frozen objects and enemies. Hold to turn nearby Krafted enemies Kineticized.**

**Hold until the snap to leave nearby enemies you marked Krafted Kineticized for 4 seconds. If an enemy is piloting, the snap freezes their Stand instead. The snap can be perfect blocked. The held version has a longer cooldown:**

**Base cooldown: 1.25s**

## Stepping Stones

**Throw three floating stones to cross gaps or climb higher.**

**Aim in a direction to throw out three stone slabs. Enemies can break them. Stepping on your own stones resets your jump cooldown. Placing more keeps the stone you are standing on. You can charge and launch the stones like other frozen objects.**

**Base cooldown: 5s**

## EX Moveset

## Suspended Slam (2 Bars)

**Touch the ground to create floating rocks for later use.**

**The ground erupts 2 seconds after the touch, knocking enemies upwards and away from the blast, and creating three floating rocks. Tap werk to trigger it early. You can stand on the rocks and use them as projectiles, but enemies can break them.**

**Base cooldown: 18s**

![slam.gif](https://trello.com/1/cards/6ad7d76df72c581dfcbbcf35/attachments/6ae084a3192c49ad1e3a813f/previews/6ae084ab192c49ad1e3a8aad/download/slam.gif)

**(Using Suspended Slam and then charging the rocks via M1s and eventually releasing with Werk.)**

## Tapping With Malicious Intent (1 Bar)

**Kraft Work teleports between up to 5 of your frozen objects and taps them toward a Krafted enemy.**

**Aim at an enemy you left Krafted. Kraft Work teleports between up to 5 nearby frozen objects and taps them toward that enemy, keeping their stored charge. Cannot be used while piloting.**

**Base cooldown: 12s**

![tapping.gif](https://trello.com/1/cards/6ad7d76df72c581dfcbbcf35/attachments/6ae0854ff2feb317851ff5bf/previews/6ae08551f2feb317851ffd77/download/tapping.gif)

**(Launching five fixated rocks towards a Krafted enemy.)**

## Kinetic Field (3 Bars)

**Create a field for 10 seconds that freezes projectiles, loose throwables, vehicles and piloted Stands, and leaves enemies Krafted.**

**For 10 seconds, a kinetic field surrounds Kraft Work. It freezes projectiles and loose throwables, trains and piloted Stands for 4 seconds. Enemies inside become Krafted. More ability makes the field larger. You can move and attack while it is active.**

**Base cooldown: 30s**

![field.gif](https://trello.com/1/cards/6ad7d76df72c581dfcbbcf35/attachments/6ae085f261021d44a631dee0/previews/6ae085fc61021d44a6320c5f/download/field.gif)

## Kinetic Leap (1 Bar)

**Build force in your own body, then launch through enemies.**

**On the ground, Kraft Work strikes you and launches you where you look, knocking enemies in your path aside. While piloting, punch your frozen body to charge it and aim the launch. Use R, press Kinetic Leap again, or tap Werk to launch yourself. In the air, Kraft Work lifts and freezes you for 6 seconds while you pilot the Stand. Punch your body to aim the launch. Hitting enemies during the air version deals much more damage.**

**Base cooldown: 16s**
