## Lore:

**“it is a stand that enjoys massacres.”**

**The Stand of Gray Fly, Resembling a large stag beetle, This stand moves at speeds so high it is difficult to see with the naked eye. It specializes in causing mass accidents disguised as natural disasters and attacks by tearing out the victim’s tongue.**
