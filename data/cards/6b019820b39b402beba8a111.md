## Original description

**Guard starts at 35 block HP and reaches 50 at max level. Regeneration starts 2.5 seconds after the last blocked hit and rises from 1.25 to 2.5 HP per second with level. At 166 effective Health, capacity and regeneration are both 30% higher.**

**A blocked hit spends guard based on the damage it would deal to you. An M1 combo finisher spends twice its normal damage. The opening hit of a blockable grab spends half the damage of the full grab sequence.**

**Guard sparks appear where the hit lands and fade from white toward red as guard runs low. Barrages show the color for each blocked hit. Knives, Stingy Eyes and most projectiles make half-size sparks. The hit that empties guard is absorbed, and the next guarded hit breaks it. Guard-breaking moves can break it immediately.**

**A guard break restores 50% of maximum block HP. A successful parry restores 20% of maximum block HP, up to full.**
**Against players, each attacker builds a separate combo from the health their hits actually take. After dealing 50% of your max HP, their next hit deals 25% less damage. The reduction grows to 40% once they have dealt 99%. Direct hits refresh the 2.5-second window. Chip and damage over time count toward an active combo but cannot extend it.**

**Your current HP scales ordinary walking and sprinting. You move at 100% of your normal speed at full HP, 82% speed at half HP, and 65% speed at 1% HP or below. Healing raises your speed along the same curve.**

**A normal dash near the end of a light attack, or at the start of the next M1 in the string, lets you keep the next punch. A fresh dash at the start of the opening M1 can still cancel it.**

**During a gang war, posing charges EX only while you have an active combat tag from an opponent. Joining the match or taking environmental damage alone earns no pose EX.**
