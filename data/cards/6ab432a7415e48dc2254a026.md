## Lore:

# **“I’ll be the judge!”**

**The Stand of Jotaro Kujo. One of the most powerful Close-Range Stands, it possesses superhuman senses, strength, and speed. Its overwhelming power is capable of destroying diamonds, and it eventually awakens the ability to stop time.**
