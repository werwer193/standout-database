**Until taking human form, fight as JoJosapiens with your unsummonable unformed self behind you and 15% more incoming damage, returning to this form on death.**

**Barrage ahead with blockable arcing papers that cover hit player’s screens in survey popups.**

**Base cooldown: 9s**

## Final Answer

**JoJosapiens dashes in and punches the enemy away, breaking their guard and the scenery in its path.**

**Base cooldown: 11s**

![answer.gif](https://trello.com/1/cards/6af25326efcf475a0337d6a8/attachments/6af87b67dddcb93d94b7b197/previews/6af87b6adddcb93d94b7b3ee/download/answer.gif)

## Loose Papers

**Throw up to two paper bundles for 15 seconds each to trip and knock down the first enemy stepping on them, with anyone able to pick them up and throw them elsewhere.**

**Base cooldown: 6s**

![paper.gif](https://trello.com/1/cards/6af25326efcf475a0337d6a8/attachments/6af87bdd0c0bdf8ab6cb539a/previews/6af87be20c0bdf8ab6cb577a/download/paper.gif)

## Questionnare (IN COMBAT)

**Answer a combat JoJo question by clicking one of three answers or pressing its skill key within 8 seconds for 2 Data, starting with three skips and recovering one per three correct answers up to three.**

**Base cooldown: 20s**

## Data

**Earn 4 Data per right answer or 2 Data per full combo against the same enemy once every 8 seconds, excluding allies, your summons and practice targets**

**Keep data when dismissing your Stand, but lose it on death or a Stand change.**

![image.webp](https://trello.com/1/cards/6af25326efcf475a0337d6a8/attachments/6af87e884135235bfcc6d29e/previews/6af87e884135235bfcc6d372/download/image.webp)

## Survey Upgrades

**Every 3 Data grants one of fourteen upgrade picks from three options, strengthening repeated choices, with details on hover under the meter and any unchosen picks randomized upon becoming Ultimate JoJo. (You can have up to 14 Upgrades.)**

![image.webp](https://trello.com/1/cards/6af25326efcf475a0337d6a8/attachments/6af87ee7e630aadb4a48b752/previews/6af87ee7e630aadb4a48b872/download/image.webp)

## Imperfect JoJo (1 Bar)

**Spend 1 EX bar to take your human form early and release JoJosapiens beside you, enabling survey upgrades and 5% more damage until continuous EX drain empties the gauge or you press again.**

**Base cooldown: 8s**

![imperfecttt.gif](https://trello.com/1/cards/6af25326efcf475a0337d6a8/attachments/6b0498430582c664eb6cbf3a/previews/6b0498450582c664eb6cc236/download/imperfecttt.gif)

## Ultimate JoJo (3 Bars)

**With 42 Data, transform from unformed or Imperfect JoJo into the Ultimate JoJo Human until death, wearing your Ultimate look with JoJosapiens beside you, gaining 15% damage and 10% speed, activating every survey upgrade and ending Data and questions.**

**Base cooldown: 45s**

![ultimates.gif](https://trello.com/1/cards/6af25326efcf475a0337d6a8/attachments/6b0497e4da1f7117fa723532/previews/6b0497e8da1f7117fa723f05/download/ultimates.gif)
