## 200 Balloons: Inflate's Slam variant creates 1 more balloon per 30 invested ability points, up to 5 extra. Total Balloons' EX meter drain is reduced by up to 1/3rd with ability investment

## Beyond The Sea: Jellyfish Summon and Humu Summon lifespan scales

## Cat Size: Ankle Snare's lifetime and trap duration scale with ability, up to 50% longer.

## Catch The Rainbow: Motionless Rain creates 1 additional drop per arm for every 50 ability points invested.

## Emperor: Hol Horse Philosophy time limit and Blind Shot bullet lifespan scale with ability, up to 50% longer. Emperor Pulemet's movement penalty scales with ability, removing up to 35% of the movement penalty.

## Glory Days: Mag size scales with ability, increasing by 1 for every 30 points.

## Harvest: Search Party's scouting interval scales and maximum finds scale with ability, up to 50% faster intervals and +3 max finds. All harvest poison damage scales with ability, dealing up to 50% more per tick.

## Heaven's Door: Written Command's special effects from "Forget Motion" "Forget Me" and "Lower Your Guard" have their duration scale with ability, up to 50% longer. Safety Lock scales with ability, preventing up to 3 additional attacks.

## Hermit Purple: Hermit's Sling search and travel range scales with ability, up to 50% further.

## John Rod: Blind the Heavens' charge speed scales with ability, charging up to 33% faster

## Magician's Red: Crossfire Hurricane's projectile range scales with ability, up to 50% further. With Life Detector, the homing detection range, homing duration, and homing turn speed scale with ability, up to 50% longer/faster. Life Detector's radius and lifespan scales with ability, up to 50% larger radius/longer lifespan.

## Rolling Stones: Super Glue's healing and duration scale with ability, up to 50%. Rolling Stones begins moving sooner scaling with ability, up to 33% sooner. Rolling Stones' "Within Death's Reach" triggers earlier scaling with ability, from 10 seconds to a max of 15 seconds.

## Smoke On The Water: Boil duration and Pressure Cooker duration scales off ability, up to 50% longer. Puddle size from Super Soaker and Hot Potato scales off ability, up to 50% wider/deeper puddles. Heat self damage scales off ability, up to 1/3rd less self damage.

## Smooth Operators: All special effects from the EX moves have their duration scale with ability, up to 50% longer.

## Super Fly: Damage Redirection's max damage stored scales with ability, from 120 up to a max of 180.

## The Grateful Dead: Withering Mist range and aging stacks per tick scales with ability, up to 50% larger range and stacks applied per tick. Senior Citizen EX meter gain and age stacks scale off ability, up to 50% more meter gain and 33% less aging stacks. Ice Pack duration and unclaimed ice lifespan scale off ability, up to 50% longer.

## The Sun: The Sun's model size scales with ability, capped at twice its normal size.

## Tower of Gray: Acid Spit fires an additional glob per 30 points in ability, up to 5 extra.

## Tubular Bells: Inflate Balloons' balloon slots increase by 1 per 30 points in ability. Pick Up Scent duration, Balloon Flower duration, and Balloon Parade duration scale off ability, up to 50%

## Under World: Burrow and Unearth's Warrior lifespan scale with ability, up to 50% longer. Unearth and Prop Memory's wind ups shorten by up to 1/3rd. Every 30 ability points invested adds an additional Tora side bomb to Tora! Tora! Tora!

## Walking Heart: Highest Heels duration scales with ability, from 10 seconds up to a max of 15 seconds. Heel length and other moves remain their fixed values.

## West End Girl: Money Case lifespan and Gap Traverse range scale with ability, up to 50% longer.
