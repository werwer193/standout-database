## Original description

## **Lore:**

**“Anything is possible if i use Under World!”**

**The Stand of Donatello Versus. A long-range Stand bound to the earth itself, Under World possesses the terrifying ability to dig up and revive past events from the ground. Anything that once happened on a patch of land, a falling boulder, a passing car, a victim’s last breath no matter what it is it can be unearthed and replayed in the present.**
