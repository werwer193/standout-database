# Lore:

# “You feel ‘guilty’, right?”

**Tamami Kobayashi tricks people into feeling guilty. His Stand appears on their chest as a padlock. The guiltier they feel, the heavier it gets.**

## Lock Spin

**Wind up and swing both locks around you. The last hit sends enemies in the direction you’re facing. While this is on cooldown, your heavy attack works as usual.**

**Base cooldown 10s**

## Iron Lock Barrage

**Quickly barrage forward with punches from your hand locks. Lunge forward on the last punch and knock the target away.**

**Damage scales 70% with Strength and 30% with Ability.**

**Base cooldown 16s**

![barragess.gif](https://trello.com/1/cards/6b018511f4da56a11e82f924/attachments/6b01efae4628d00783f879b6/previews/6b01efb24628d00783f87ebe/download/barragess.gif)

## Weight of Guilt

**The lock gauntlets deal 20% more light punch damage and 12% more heavy punch damage than bare fists. Both reach 12% farther. With the gauntlets out, taking 8 direct damage from the same enemy puts a lock on them.**

**The meter on their chest shows their Guilt. The lock grows at 30, 50, 80 and 100 Guilt. When the wearer hits you, their lock sends 35%, 55%, 70%, 85% or 95% of the damage back to them, depending on its size. Larger locks slow them more. At 100 Guilt, Crushing Guilt stops their dash. The inside of the meter fills as long as they stay at 100. When it fills, they collapse. This takes 25 seconds for most wearers and a full minute for bosses. Training dummies are exempt.**

![image.webp](https://trello.com/1/cards/6b018511f4da56a11e82f924/attachments/6b01f1b1f6d1f90360f02e82/previews/6b01f1b2f6d1f90360f02f3c/download/image.webp)

## Sob Story

**Tell a sad story to stagger enemies within 30 studs, even through block. Each enemy hit gains 15 Guilt.**

**Base cooldown 14s**

![sob.gif](https://trello.com/1/cards/6b018511f4da56a11e82f924/attachments/6b01f05815e6281fa4538498/previews/6b01f05b15e6281fa45385e7/download/sob.gif)

## Guilt Trip

**Tense up in fear of being hit. Catch a direct hit within 0.85 seconds to take no damage and fly backwards. The attacker gains Guilt equal to the damage you would have taken, with a minimum of 10. Miss and you’re open for 0.22 seconds. Catching a hit halves the cooldown.**

**Base cooldown 10s**

![counters.gif](https://trello.com/1/cards/6b018511f4da56a11e82f924/attachments/6b01f12def808dccb4ce806c/previews/6b01f132ef808dccb4ce86aa/download/counters.gif)

## Lock Toss

**Lift a giant lock overhead and throw it where you aim. A direct hit knocks the target back, ragdolls them and gives them a concussion.**

**Base cooldown 12s**

![toss.gif](https://trello.com/1/cards/6b018511f4da56a11e82f924/attachments/6b01f167e2cce29fd706f429/previews/6b01f16ae2cce29fd706f65f/download/toss.gif)

## Pardon

**Aim at a wearer within 35 studs and press G to open their lock. You can lock as many enemies as you like. When you hit someone wearing your lock, they lose Guilt. Your light punches take off 2, heavies take off 8 and other direct hits take off 4 to 8. After 13 seconds without gaining Guilt, the wearer loses 9 per second. Putting your gauntlets away opens all your locks.**

![pardon.gif](https://trello.com/1/cards/6b018511f4da56a11e82f924/attachments/6b01f224f90388044583724c/previews/6b01f228f903880445838205/download/pardon.gif)

# EX Moves (Awakening Moves)

## Heavy Conscience (2 Bars)

**Point ahead to slam any enemy wearing a lock into the ground. The smallest lock holds them down for 1 second. Each larger size adds half a second and 6 damage, up to a 3-second hold. A perfect block stops the slam.**

**Base cooldown: 20s**

![lock.gif](https://trello.com/1/cards/6b018511f4da56a11e82f924/attachments/6b01f2b73f46ae6440993c6c/previews/6b01f2c03f46ae64409949f8/download/lock.gif)

## Settlement (1 Bar)

**Call a lock wearer over to pay 20 Dalas per lock size, up to 100. Players pay what they have available. NPCs pay the full amount. Once they pay, their lock comes off and their Guilt resets to 0.**

**Base cooldown: 24s**

![money.gif](https://trello.com/1/cards/6b018511f4da56a11e82f924/attachments/6b01f378615a016daf46cccd/previews/6b01f37b615a016daf46d2ec/download/money.gif)

## Lock and Roll (2 Bars)

**At 30 Guilt, pin a lock wearer to a nearby prop, lamp post or vehicle. The hold starts at 3.5 seconds and gains 3.5 seconds each time the lock grows. Ability extends the hold. Their Guilt stops decaying while pinned. Pardon releases them early. You can drive away with them attached.**

**Base cooldown: 25s**

![locks.gif](https://trello.com/1/cards/6b018511f4da56a11e82f924/attachments/6b01f556112cb72aae10fd44/previews/6b01f566112cb72aae110dc9/download/locks.gif)

## Schrodinger's Cat (1 Bar)

**Set down a box that may or may not contain a cat. The first enemy who damages it or drives over it gains 50 Guilt. The box lasts 2 minutes. Placing another replaces it.**

**Base cooldown: 20s**

![cats.gif](https://trello.com/1/cards/6b018511f4da56a11e82f924/attachments/6b01f57dc0ab4a689897808b/previews/6b01f586c0ab4a6898978b93/download/cats.gif)
