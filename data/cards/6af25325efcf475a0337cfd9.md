## Original description

## Lore:

**Ningbo’s Stand can inflate anything he touches, including parts of a person’s body. The affected area becomes light enough to float and fragile enough to burst. A firefighter working with Laem Chabang, Ningbo uses this ability to threaten people up close. He can also inflate bullets to keep them from hurting him.**

## Inflating Attacks

**Your own light attacks apply inflations. TOTAL BALLOONS doubles their inflations while active.**

![inflates.gif](https://trello.com/1/cards/6aba7aec9f5cc34ba1b5c06d/attachments/6abfb315d83c73f6a7336e7b/previews/6abfb31bd83c73f6a7337858/download/inflates.gif)

## Sudden Balloons

**Grab an enemy and rapidly inflate their body before dropping them.**

**Your character performs the grab. The hit lands partway through the opening animation and can be blocked. A caught enemy is inflated repeatedly, then released and ragdolled.**

**Base cooldown: 20s**

![sudden.gif](https://trello.com/1/cards/6aba7aec9f5cc34ba1b5c06d/attachments/6abfb36996df47062e71c375/previews/6abfb36c96df47062e71c677/download/sudden.gif)

## Inflate

**Inflate an enemy or turn a loose object into a exploding balloon. Hold to slam the ground and inflate its debris.**

**Tap on an enemy for random body inflations, or inflate a nearby loose prop into a throwable balloon. Hold for the ground slam; some debris becomes balloons that drift outward and upward, then fade. Grab them with H before they disappear. Inflated gas cans make a large gasoline puddle on impact; Hydrants spew water when inflated and make a water puddle when thrown.**

**Base cooldown: 14s**

## Relentless Counter

**Counter an attack, grab the attacker by their face, and repeatedly inflate their head. Bursts their head if they finish below 20% health.**

**Incoming projectiles become balloons while you hold the counter pose. A succesful counter holds the ragdolled attacker by their face and repeatedly inflates their head; it bursts if their final health is below 20%.**

**Base cooldown: 28s**

## EX [Awakening Moves]

## TOTAL BALLOONS [1 Bar]

**Spend 1 EX bar to surround yourself with balloons. Attackers receive an inflation, M1S cause twice as many inflations, and incoming projectiles become balloons without blocking. Drains 5% of your full EX meter each second.**

**Base cooldown: 3s**
