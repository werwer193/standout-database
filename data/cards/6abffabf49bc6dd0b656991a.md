## BEING REWORKED

Lore:

**“If you people came out of the crevices, then go right back into them!! Get crushed into little pieces!!”**

**The Stand of Key West. West End Girl can inhabit and traverse through gaps of virtually any size, using even the narrowest of gaps as vantage points & pathways. From these openings, it can extend its numerous hands to attack, restrain, or manipulate objects.**

## Crushing Pressure [GAP/ITEM/ID]

**Aim at an enemy within 24 studs. A gap within 16 studs of them lets the hands grab the targets and enemies within 12 studs of the emergence point, then deal 35 damage each. The 3.5 second hold halves for every additional successful grab.**

**Base cooldown: 14s**

## Ankle Snare

**Aim at an enemy within 24 studs with a gap within 16 studs of them. Hands pin their ankles for 5 seconds, then deal 25 damage and break a leg for 8 seconds. Unblockable. Or aim at a loose throwable near a gap and an enemy to hurl it at the nearest enemy.**

**Base cooldown: 18s**

## Money Case

**Tap to throw a money case at your aim. Hold for six close-range hits; the final hit knocks enemies back. Money bursts from every hit and impact. A dropped case leaves a gap West End Girl can use for 25 seconds.**

**Base cooldown: 14s**
