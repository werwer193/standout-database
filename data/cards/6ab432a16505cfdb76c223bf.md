## Zoom Punch

**Strike the nearest enemy in front of you within reach, briefly stunning them and knocking them back. The punch can be blocked or parried and cannot reach through walls. Throwing it costs 2 health, but cannot reduce you below 1 health.**

**Base cooldown: 12s**

![zoom.gif](https://trello.com/1/cards/6ab432a16505cfdb76c223bf/attachments/6abbabacc3404becd09c6475/previews/6abbabb0c3404becd09c674b/download/zoom.gif)

## Hamonic Breath

**While Hamonic lasts, you recover health, dash farther and infuse light attacks with Hamon, A fuller bar gives stronger healing and dashes. Breathe again to top it up. At full charge, the bar waits three seconds before draining. Taking a hit stops your breath, but keeps the Hamonic you already built. Effects that prevent healing still apply.**

**Base cooldown: 1s**
