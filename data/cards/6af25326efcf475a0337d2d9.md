## Lore:

# “The house itself is a ghost.”

**The stand of Emporio Alnino. Burning Down The House lets him use the ghosts of destroyed rooms and objects. He hides in the remains of a music room that burned down at Green Dolphin Street Prison, entering through a narrow crack in the wall. The room gives Emporio and his friends a place to hide.**

## Grand Piano

**Use the piano’s prompt to sit down. Play Giorno’s Theme without a wrong note to give everyone in the room 30% more damage for 60 seconds. Inspired does not stack.**

![image.webp](https://trello.com/1/cards/6acb7b2331891312381375a5/attachments/6acbd9d7489de9d1bdd71fa0/previews/6acbd9d7489de9d1bdd72079/download/image.webp)

**(You can use the sheet)**

## Ghost Room

**Hold to choose a wall, then release to open a crack. Keep your hand against the wall for 3 seconds. Use again to close it when nobody is inside. Your room and stored items stay until you die.**

**Base cooldown: 8s**

# EX [Awakening Moves]

## Temporary Doorway [3 Bars]

**Open a smaller doorway into your room without charging, It lasts 5 seconds. Your original entrance closes while it is open, then reopens. Create your room first.**

**Base cooldown: 25s**

![temp.gif](https://trello.com/1/cards/6acb7b2331891312381375a5/attachments/6acbd61652961f894bf662b1/previews/6acbd61d52961f894bf66bef/download/temp.gif)

## Ghost Pallet

**Place a wooden pallet to climb on or cross a gap. Keep up to 3 at once. Each lasts 12 seconds and can be broken.**

**Base cooldown: 6s**

![ghost.gif](https://trello.com/1/cards/6acb7b2331891312381375a5/attachments/6acbd6753962712dc7501cbd/previews/6acbd6793962712dc7501e50/download/ghost.gif)

**Base cooldown: 10s**
