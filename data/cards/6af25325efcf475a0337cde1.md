## Lore:

**“YES, I AM!”**

**The Stand of Muhammed Avdol. A powerful Close-Range Stand that generates and manipulates intense flames. It can shape fire into various forms, such as the Red Bind or Crossfire Hurricane, and is capable of incinerating anything it touches.**

# EX [Awakening Moves]

## Red Bind [3 Bars]

**Your stand catches enemies, lifts them into the air, then slams them toward you. You can still move and attack. While piloting, enemies are thrown toward your Stand instead.**

![redbind.gif](https://trello.com/1/cards/6ab4356f469bbaad7ce157b8/attachments/6ab538bf4fae27737fba8256/previews/6ab538c84fae27737fba8bc9/download/redbind.gif)

## Life Detector [1 Bar]

**Mark nearby living enemies within 80 studs so Crossfire Hurricane can track them.**

**Place the detector to mark nearby living enemies. While you are near it, Crossfire Hurricane can track the marked target closest to your aim.**

**Base cooldown: 25s**

![oks.gif](https://trello.com/1/cards/6af25325efcf475a0337cde1/attachments/6af8acf5cf3c4d783083cdb7/previews/6af8acf9cf3c4d783083d403/download/oks.gif)

## Flame Breath [1 Bar]

**Hold: breathe fire while walking for 1 EX bar upfront and 0.75 EX bars per second, ending on release or empty EX**

**Hits: every 0.5 seconds, including ragdolled enemies, refreshing a 1-second knockdown with half damage on follow-up hits against ragdolled enemies.**

**Blocking: greatly reduces damage and stops knockback while still allowing burn.**

**Ability scaling: Damage scales 90% with Ability and 10% with Strength, and Ability increases the flame and its hitbox from 90% to 145% of the original size at 166 Ability, and its burn lasts 3 to 8 seconds with Ability.**

**Base cooldown: 25s**

![breath.gif](https://trello.com/1/cards/6af25325efcf475a0337cde1/attachments/6af8b28cf02475ff974afefd/previews/6af8b294f02475ff974b046b/download/breath.gif)
