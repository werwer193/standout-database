## Original description

## Lore:

**“Someone has to stay.”**

**The Stand of Toyohiro Kanedaichi, bound to the transmission tower he calls home. Anyone left alone inside becomes its prisoner, turning to metal if they try to leave without a replacement. Attacking the tower is no way out: it stores the force of each blow and sends it back.**

## Prisoner Boundary

**If you are the tower’s lone prisoner, crossing outside its boundary turns you to metal. Another occupant is needed before the prisoner can leave normally. Attacking the tower feeds Damage Redirection.**

![metal.gif](https://trello.com/1/cards/6ab4353cad1e1994b19837e6/attachments/6ab5163fc83d9a13607b6794/previews/6ab51646c83d9a13607b6d66/download/metal.gif)

## Deploy Power Line

**Throw a power line where you aim. Anyone can click near the top to zip down, or near the landed hook to climb back up. Click again to let go.**

**Tap to deploy a line. Any player can aim at the top and press M1 nearby to ride down, or do the same at the bottom to climb up more slowly. Press M1 again while riding to drop.**

**Base cooldown: 12s**

![powerline.gif](https://trello.com/1/cards/6ab4353cad1e1994b19837e6/attachments/6ab51363c16232049e420f2f/previews/6ab51367c16232049e4213eb/download/powerline.gif)

## Screw Volley

**Fire three screws from the tower. Shiftlock aims them at the target closest to your mouse. Screws stick into whatever they hit.**

**Base cooldown: 23s**

![rods.gif](https://trello.com/1/cards/6ab4353cad1e1994b19837e6/attachments/6ab513f89224b9998233b13d/previews/6ab513fb9224b9998233b4ab/download/rods.gif)

## Damage Redirection

**Super Fly stores damage from attacks against it. Tap to fire it at your target, or hold to send each attacker their share back. You can hit the tower to charge it yourself. While someone else is trapped, it fires back automatically.**

**Base cooldown: 15s**

![retaliation.gif](https://trello.com/1/cards/6ab4353cad1e1994b19837e6/attachments/6ab51b3ee234a8b05d33aa62/previews/6ab51b48e234a8b05d33b4c5/download/retaliation.gif)

## Super Leap

**Hold to pick a ledge on your tower, then release to jump there.**

**Base cooldown: 35s**

![jump.gif](https://trello.com/1/cards/6ab4353cad1e1994b19837e6/attachments/6ab514ac8e88ef9acb452351/previews/6ab514b18e88ef9acb452a67/download/jump.gif)

# EX [Awakening Moves]

## Tower Reposition

**Send the whole tower spinning into the sky, then bring it down where you’re standing. The landing sends nearby enemies flying and plants Super Fly in its new spot.**

**Base cooldown: 75s**
