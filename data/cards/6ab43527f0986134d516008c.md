## Original description

## Lore:

**“The gun is mightier than the sword!”**

**The stand of Hol Horse. A gun summoned directly to the user’s hand, Emperor allows its user to fire bullets that are also part of the Stand. These bullets can be steered mid-air with absolute precision, making cover useless.**

## Hol Horse Philosophy [HOLD]

**Hold** to run forward quickly, then **release** to stop.

Start running in the direction you face. Keep **holding** to run, or **release** to stop. **Being stunned or reaching the time limit** also **ends** the run.

**Base cooldown: 55s**

![RUN.gif](https://trello.com/1/cards/6ab43527f0986134d516008c/attachments/6ab4434b4f75941ce0701f87/previews/6ab443554f75941ce07027f7/download/RUN.gif)

## Blind Shots

Fire a group of bullets and guide them by moving your **camera.**

Fire a group of bullets, then turn the **camera** to steer them. You **stay in place** while controlling them. Each bullet **dissapears** after a hit. **Control ends when no bullets remain, the group hits a wall, or six seconds pass.**

‌

**Base cooldown: 35s**

![blindshots.gif](https://trello.com/1/cards/6ab43527f0986134d516008c/attachments/6ab444ed6ad8045716b392e2/download/blindshots.gif)

## Fan The Hammer [HOLD]

**Tap** to fire **ricocheting** bullets, or **hold** for a close-range volley that hits **instantly**.

**VARIATION: [HOLD]**

**Tap** to fire a short volley of bullets that can **bounce off surfaces. Hold** through the **startup** to fire more slowly, **hitting instantly** at shorter range. Both versions shoot in the **direction you face.**

**Base cooldown: 23s**

## Emperor Shot [HOLD]

**Charge** a blast straight ahead, **Holding** increases its **damage,size,reach and knockback.**

**VARIATION: [HOLD]**
**Hold** to **charge** for up to two seconds, then **release** to fire. **A full charge doubles the blast size, damage and knockback.** The shot travels in the **direction you face**. **You move slowly and cannot jump while charging, and firing pushes you backward.**

**Base cooldown: 28s**

# EX [Awakening Moves]

## Emperor Pulemet

Requires 4 bars of [https://trello.com/c/Kus9CS84/50-awakening-bar](https://trello.com/c/Kus9CS84/50-awakening-bar "smartCard-inline")
**Charge up**, then keep firing across a **wide area in front** of you.

**After a short charge**, fire continously for nearly **four seconds.** You **cannot move** while firing, but you can **turn** to aim. **Repeated hits push enemies back and wear down their guard.**

**Base cooldown: 120s**
