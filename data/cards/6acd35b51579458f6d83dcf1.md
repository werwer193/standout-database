## “I’ll be the judge!”

**The Stand of Jotaro Kujo, now known as Star Platinum: The World. It  retains Star Platinum’s strength,speed, and precision, along with the ability to stop time that Jotaro discovered during his fight with DIO**.

## EX: [Awakening Moveset]

## Campfire

**Build a campfire in front of your Stand. Interact to sit and recover health, even during combat. Its warmth dries you off, but stepping into the flames burns and briefly stuns you.**

**Build on flat ground in front of your Stand. Including while piloting. Interact to rest and recover 3 health each second, even in combat. Interact again or jump to get up. Taking damage ends your rest. Its warmth dries you off and makes aging worse.**

**The fire lasts 10 seconds. Anyone touching the flames takes damage, briefly stumbles, and catches fire, including you. Attacks, liquid moves, and uncovered rain can put it out. During Time Stop, it waits to ignite until time resumes. Costs no EX.**

**Base cooldown: 20s**

![campfire.gif](https://trello.com/1/cards/6acd35b51579458f6d83dcf1/attachments/6acd3b2b4f4c39d9a21539c9/previews/6acd3b2f4f4c39d9a2154613/download/campfire.gif)

## Tandem Rushdown [2 Bars]

**Star Platinum rushes an enemy from the front, barrages them, then slams them into the ground. You can move and attack once it sets off.**

**Aim at an enemy within 40 studs. The rush can be blocked or parried and ends if you are interrupted or the target gets away. Cannot be used while piloting.**

**Costs 2 EX bars.**

![rushdown.gif](https://trello.com/1/cards/6acd35b51579458f6d83dcf1/attachments/6acd3c31e174c0ae35073cd5/previews/6acd3c34e174c0ae3507416e/download/rushdown.gif)

## Panic Time Stop / Short [TAP] /  Full [HOLD]

**Tap for a short Time Stop, or hold for the full duration. Both reach 150 studs. Use it during another player’s Time Stop to move again.**

**Tap for an instant stop, even with your Stand dismissed, for 1 EX bar. With your Stand summoned, hold to spend 4 on a longer stop with windup. A short stop has a 20s cooldown; a full stop has a 60s cooldown. Using it during someone else’s Time Stop lets you move; a large enough Ability advantage lets yours take over.**

**ABILITY SCALING: At 1 Ability, a tap lasts 2 seconds and a hold lasts 3.5 seconds. At 100 Ability, both last twice as long.**
