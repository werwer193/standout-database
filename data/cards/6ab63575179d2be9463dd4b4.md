## Original description

## Lore:

**“This is what we’re going to slide. Do it, Smooth Operators!”**

**The Stand of Dragona Joestar. A colony of four tiny operators that travel on tank treads. Working together, they can slide objects and body features across surfaces, shifting everything from a face’s features to an injury. Their strength lies in careful teamwork rather than direct fighting.**

## Injury Shift / Target Yourself [HOLD]

**Lift an injury, healing up to 10/15/20/25% max health with 1/2/3/4 operators. Use again to pass that percentage to another target as damage, or destroy a nearby prop or dropped item. Hold to target yourself. Getting hit or having the transfer blocked returns the injury to its original owner.**

**Base cooldown: 14s**

## Rearrange

**Shift an enemy' body for 8 seconds. One operator targets a leg, two the eyes, three both, and four add an arm. Legs slow movement, eyes blur vision, and arms weaken damage.**

**Base cooldown: 16s**

# EX [Awakening Moves]

## Face Lift [2 Bars]

**Lift someone’s face markings for this life. Use again on another person to apply them, hold either use to target yourself, or aim at empty ground to discard them. Carried markings disappear after 20 seconds.**

**Base cooldown: 18s**

## Zip It [2 Bars]

**Slide an enemy’s mouth shut, preventing them from chatting for 25 seconds.**

**Base cooldown: 24s**

![zip.gif](https://trello.com/1/cards/6ab63575179d2be9463dd4b4/attachments/6ab64714cc8f52e52a73f665/download/zip.gif)

## Lock Up [2 Bars]

**Send Smooth Operators to seal a nearby door shut for 15 seconds.**

**Base cooldown: 25s**

![lock.gif](https://trello.com/1/cards/6ab63575179d2be9463dd4b4/attachments/6ab647e2f5439f0d05407291/previews/6ab647e5f5439f0d054076fa/download/lock.gif)

## ISHI! ISHI! ISHI! [3 Bars]

**All four operators rush an enemy and steal their health HUD for 10 seconds. Requires the full colony on the ground.**

**Base cooldown: 35s**

![ishishi.gif](https://trello.com/1/cards/6ab63575179d2be9463dd4b4/attachments/6abc47ff0c7d7ff534c1399c/previews/6abc48030c7d7ff534c13df1/download/ishishi.gif)
