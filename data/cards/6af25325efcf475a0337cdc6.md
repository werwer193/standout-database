# Lore:

# **“I… have the ability to catch the rainbow.”**

**The stand of Blackmore. A mask-like Stand that grants the user the ability to manipulate rain. He can freeze raindrops in mid-air to walk upon them or use them as deadly, razor-sharp projectiles.**

## Catch The Rainbow

**Catch The Rainbow needs fog, rain, or an active hydrant within 30 studs to be used.**

## Rain Walking/Floating

**Summon your stand, then hold Space for a short moment to begin rising. Release Jump to keep your height and move horizontally. Hold Z to descend. Sprinting makes horizontal floating faster. Landing ends the float; release and press Jump again after a dive.**

![float.gif](https://trello.com/1/cards/6ab43547aecedbc970a3af11/attachments/6ab53ac85ac7748c10321282/previews/6ab53acd5ac7748c1032138d/download/float.gif)

## Hidden Controls

**While floating, E activates Rain Transport. While hidden, aim at an enemy and press M1 for its teleport follow-up. With the mask on, Ctrl launches Body Projectile. Both extra moves are listed in Moves.**

# Weapons Of Rain

**Fire seven water shots that soak enemies on impact.**

**Aim at your target before casting. The rain bursts also apply water to fire and gasoline they hit**.

**Base cooldown: 23s**

![raindropfix.gif](https://trello.com/1/cards/6ab43547aecedbc970a3af11/attachments/6ab53f0118103f44f7bcbfe3/previews/6ab53f0518103f44f7bccae6/download/raindropfix.gif)

## Motionless Rain

**Surround yourself with suspended raindrops for projectile defense.**

**Your mask must be on. The raindrops stay around you until  used up; wait for them to clear before creating another set.**

**Base cooldown: 45s**

![raindrooops.gif](https://trello.com/1/cards/6ab43547aecedbc970a3af11/attachments/6ab53e5b558de78af70ca5f2/previews/6ab53e5e558de78af70ca8eb/download/raindrooops.gif)

## Triple Shot

**Fire three shots toward your mouse.**

**Base cooldown: 27s**

![tripleshot.gif](https://trello.com/1/cards/6af25325efcf475a0337cdc6/attachments/6afa137050ec549284a9fb5d/previews/6afa137350ec549284a9fdd4/download/tripleshot.gif)

## Rain Catch

**Leap up and press again in the air to dive, or dive at once while airborne, catching an enemy through ordinary guard and executing a weakened target.**

**Can be perfect blocked.**

**Base cooldown: 32s**
