## Original description

## Lore:

## “Got him! He took the bait!”

**The stand of Pesci, a member of La Squadra Esecuzioni. Beach Boy is a fishing rod whose line and hook pass through anything but their prey. Any attack on the line travels into whoever is hooked.**

## Ability Scaling

**Ability makes your line longer and your reel stronger. The line can reach up to 225 studs.**

## For My Bro!

**Anyone who kills a member of your party is marked for 20 seconds. Your hook sinks into them 50% faster.**

## Cast Out (Ctrl)

**Steer your hook through walls and floors to catch someone. Use again to stop steering, then again to resume. Heavy attacks reels it back.**

**Base cooldown: 1s**

![reel.gif](https://trello.com/1/cards/6ad1d959c077dd6581a2e4ac/attachments/6ad1ef9c47abef2f2d048c74/previews/6ad1efa247abef2f2d049d3e/download/reel.gif)

## The Line Carries

**You and your allies can hit the line to damage whoever is hooked. Hamon also travels through it.**

## An Angler’s Hold

**Being ragdolled or hit with heavy knockback releases whoever you have hooked, including during Focused Fishing.**

## Big Catches

**Hook a large object, such as a car. Hold M1 to reel yourself toward it, or press R for a quick pull.**

## Quickcast

**Cast your hook toward your aim in a shallow arc. It catches people, sticks into large objects, or stays where it lands.**

## Hook Depth

**Hold M1 to reel someone in and build depth. More Strength makes the hook sink faster. Deeper hooks slow them more and deal more damage when pulled out. At 25% depth or more, R tears the hook out. They escape if they get too far beyond your line's reach.**

## EX [Awakening Moves]

## **Focused Fishing**

**Hold to reel your catch in faster and sink the hook deeper. You cannot move it while using it, and it drains EX.**

**Base cooldown: 3s**

![focused.gif](https://trello.com/1/cards/6ad1d959c077dd6581a2e4ac/attachments/6ad1f9eb6f24d3616ea6d404/previews/6ad1f9f16f24d3616ea6d995/download/focused.gif)

## Fatal Resolve [1 Bar]

**Stomp in front of you to finish downed enemies and knock standing enemies away.**

**Available with your Stand summoned or dismissed. Uses its own input.**

**Base cooldown: 8s**

## Fish Filet [2 Bars]

**Try to tear out an eye or limb, with a higher chance at greater depth. Targeting the heart causes bleeding and finishes enemies at 20% health  or less once depth reaches 100%. Can be perfect blocked.**

**Base cooldown: 14s**
