## Scent

**Scented enemies appear on your scent list and are marked through walls. Pursuit can chase them within 600 studs. Becoming Soaked washes the scent off.**

![image.webp](https://trello.com/1/cards/6adbbc892110803ccd561a60/attachments/6adfcdc2edeb46d984115c86/previews/6adfcdc2edeb46d984115d17/download/image.webp)

## Barrage

**Unleash a rapid flurry of punches on your opponent.**

**Base cooldown: 14s**

![barrages.gif](https://trello.com/1/cards/6adbbc892110803ccd561a60/attachments/6adfce55a7a43b5540855c8d/previews/6adfce5aa7a43b5540856035/download/barrages.gif)

## Scent Trap

**Aim at a throwable to coat it in scent, or aim at the ground to throw a fake Mysterious Arrow. Touching either applies Scented for 3 minutes. The arrow also drains and slows whoever takes the bait. Traps last 1 minute, with up to one arrow and two coated items at a time.**

**Base cooldown: 30s**

## Pursuit

**Tap to send Highway Star after your selected Scented target within 600 studs. It chases them on its own and drains them on contact, healing you and filling your Nutrients. Hold to recall it, or hold while it is home to cycle through scents. A perfect block as the feet land is the only way to turn them away. Sent from a pilot, it lets you go for the hunt and stays out as the pack when it is done.**

**Base cooldown: 28s**

![feet.gif](https://trello.com/1/cards/6adbbc892110803ccd561a60/attachments/6adfcfe6f1899879f187ac87/previews/6adfcfeef1899879f187ba5a/download/feet.gif)

## Footprints

**A perfect block as Pursuit’s feet land turns them away; once they are on, only becoming Soaked or riding in a fast-moving vehicle removes them, and the same goes for the feet left by Footprint Storm.**

## Nutrient Siphon

**Highway Star grabs an enemy in front of it and drains their health to heal you. Scented enemies are held longer and gain more Withered stacks. Against a downed enemy, finish them off and recover 30% of your maximum health.**

**Base cooldown: 18s**

![siphon.gif](https://trello.com/1/cards/6adbbc892110803ccd561a60/attachments/6adfd0eff865ada38c0d444d/previews/6adfd0f7f865ada38c0d4fe1/download/siphon.gif)

## Nutrients

**Stand attacks and draining enemies fill your Nutrients, up to 100. Stored Nutrients gradually heal you while Highway Star is summoned. Below 60, you take 20% more damage and move 15% slower while it is out. At 100, your next Scent Trap or Nutrient Siphon is stronger and leaves you with 60. Nutrients start fading after 6 seconds without feeding.**

![image.webp](https://trello.com/1/cards/6adbbc892110803ccd561a60/attachments/6adfd0a7fbf87502d4fa37c5/previews/6adfd0a7fbf87502d4fa3897/download/image.webp)

## Foot Form

**Hold the summon key to split Highway Star into a pack of feet. While piloting, move 75% faster and climb walls by moving into them. You cannot punch or fly in this form. Hold the key again to change back.**

**Base cooldown: 3s**

![feets.gif](https://trello.com/1/cards/6adbbc892110803ccd561a60/attachments/6adfd1b7d493ccb0cc64b483/previews/6adfd1b9d493ccb0cc64b5df/download/feets.gif)

## Footprint Flurry

**In Foot Form, strike enemies in front of you with a flurry of feet. Hits apply Scented and occasionally drain health to heal you. The final hit knocks them back. in normal form, use Barrage instead.**

**Base cooldown: 14s**

![flurry.gif](https://trello.com/1/cards/6adbbc892110803ccd561a60/attachments/6adfd2ec170f307fe2c52a33/previews/6adfd2ef170f307fe2c530e1/download/flurry.gif)

## EX Moveset

## I’m so Handsome (2 Bars)

**Pose with Highway Star to recover 25% of your maximum health and gain 40 Nutrients. Getting hit before the pose finishes cancels both rewards.**

**Base cooldown: 70s**

![pooose.gif](https://trello.com/1/cards/6adbbc892110803ccd561a60/attachments/6adfd3e9a0f84eaf698397be/previews/6adfd3eda0f84eaf69839b72/download/pooose.gif)

## DEEEEP SNIFF (3 Bars)

**Take a deep breath to detect enemies within 1,000 studs and apply Scented for 6 minutes. Can only be used out of combat.**

**Base cooldown: 120s**

![sniff.gif](https://trello.com/1/cards/6adbbc892110803ccd561a60/attachments/6adfd480a8f777ec6d1b4144/previews/6adfd486a8f777ec6d1b504e/download/sniff.gif)

## Footprint Storm (3 Bars)

**Sweep a wave of feet in front of you. Enemies caught in it are stuck repeatedly, drained and thrown back. Scented enemies take more hits. They can dash out before the feet close in. After the attack, some feet remain attached for 4 seconds, draining and slowing them.**

**Base cooldown: 40s**

![storm.gif](https://trello.com/1/cards/6adbbc892110803ccd561a60/attachments/6adfd525f51d514789675ca8/previews/6adfd536f51d5147896778a7/download/storm.gif)
