## Lore:

# **“This is the last of my Hamon!”**

**Ceasar Zeppeli channels hamon through bubbles made with his gloves. Even at death's door, he can leave his remaining Hamon to a friend.**

## This is the last of my Hamon!

**Complete the four prompts while knocked to give up your life and leave a blood bubble. A copy of your accessories floats inside.**

**Touch it within 30 seconds to collect it. It heals them for 10 seconds and adds Ripple to their punches. Hamon users also get full breath. Vampires die if they take it.**

## Moveset:

## Bubble Launcher (You get this move when you have a stand.)

**Fire eight bubbles toward your aim. Hits briefly stun and apply Ripple. The bubbles also push loose objects away.**

**Base cooldown: 9s**

![launch.gif](https://trello.com/1/cards/6aca84ba1ea3235927b014ce/attachments/6aca9de9f93566119a8210f0/previews/6aca9debf93566119a821248/download/launch.gif)

## Bubble Cutter (You get this move when you have a stand.)

**Throw two cutters one after another in a small spread. They skim over the floor, briefly stun and cause bleeding for 4 seconds.**

**Base cooldown: 11s**

![cutter.gif](https://trello.com/1/cards/6aca84ba1ea3235927b014ce/attachments/6aca9e5f889c7e4372ac14ec/previews/6aca9e61889c7e4372ac160b/download/cutter.gif)

## The next moves are exclusive to non-stand users.

## Seated Jump [EVASIVE] [1 Bar] (You get this move when you have a stand.)

**Spring away in a seated leap.**

**Aim where you want to jump. Use it while stunned to break free.**

![steated.gif](https://trello.com/1/cards/6aca84ba1ea3235927b014ce/attachments/6acaa4f9d5df11df12ced108/previews/6acaa4fbd5df11df12ced249/download/steated.gif)

## Ripple Hypnosis

**After the grab, they walk toward your cursor for 3 seconds. Another hit freezes them. They stop at walls and ledges.**

**Base cooldown: 16s**

![hypnosis.gif](https://trello.com/1/cards/6aca84ba1ea3235927b014ce/attachments/6aca9efdcb42f1f7f78ca0a2/previews/6aca9f01cb42f1f7f78ca37d/download/hypnosis.gif)

## **Bubble Lenses / Breathe [HOLD]**

**Place up to three lenses that fire burning beams at nearby enemies. They last 25 seconds, and placing a fourth replaces the oldest. Sunlight makes their beams stronger. Hit a lens with Hamon to send beams between nearby lenses. Enemies can break them.**

**Base cooldown: 1s**

![LENS.gif](https://trello.com/1/cards/6aca84ba1ea3235927b014ce/attachments/6aca9fbb58bcf9fc3cb7926b/previews/6aca9fc558bcf9fc3cb799ac/download/LENS.gif)

## Breathe **[HOLD]**

**Hold to build your breath. While charged, you heal, dash further and add Ripple to your punches. Taking a hit stops your breathing but keeps the charge you built. Breathing has its own cooldown.**

![breathe.gif](https://trello.com/1/cards/6aca84ba1ea3235927b014ce/attachments/6acaa06bc57621dcfc42f3a4/previews/6acaa06fc57621dcfc42f7e0/download/breathe.gif)

# EX: [Awakening Moves]

## Bubble Barrier [2 Bars]

**The bubble lifts an enemy for 3 seconds and hurts them with Hamon. Another hit pops it, so teammates can break them free. It can also hold loose throwables.**

**Base cooldown: 22s**

![barrier.gif](https://trello.com/1/cards/6aca84ba1ea3235927b014ce/attachments/6acaa228743f1ba57520bd61/previews/6acaa22a743f1ba57520bfb3/download/barrier.gif)
