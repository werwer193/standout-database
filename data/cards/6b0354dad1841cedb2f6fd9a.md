## Dagger Combo

**With the dagger in hand, land your fifth M1 to cause three seconds of Bleed. Plant the dagger with Sinking Suspicion up close or Disregard at range. While it’s stuck, you fight with your fists. Get close and use Pull Free to take it back.**

![combos.gif](https://trello.com/1/cards/6b0354dad1841cedb2f6fd9a/attachments/6b03636e0acbcee09498164b/previews/6b03637a0acbcee094982bb3/download/combos.gif)

## Sinking Suspicion

**Grab an opponent through their guard and leave the dagger in them. Get close and press again to pull it free, knock them down and make them bleed. You fight unarmed and your other Dolly Dagger skills are unavailable until you get it back.**

**Base cooldown 12s**

![stabby.gif](https://trello.com/1/cards/6b0354dad1841cedb2f6fd9a/attachments/6b0363c371e3e65dcc2bb880/previews/6b0363c671e3e65dcc2bbd76/download/stabby.gif)

## Swift Stabs

**Stab twice. The second stab makes your opponent bleed and knocks them off their feet.**

**Base cooldown 9s**

![combostab.gif](https://trello.com/1/cards/6b0354dad1841cedb2f6fd9a/attachments/6b03642011a43fe781d3f8a1/previews/6b03642311a43fe781d3f96e/download/combostab.gif)

## Steadier Than Hands

**Drive Dolly Dagger into the ground below your right arm. While you stay within 12 studs, incoming attack damage goes back to whoever dealt it. The reflected hit leaves at least 1 HP. Press again within 45 studs to trigger Phantasmagoria Burst. The flash hits and briefly blinds nearby enemies. The returning dagger cuts anyone in its path. An enemy can kick it loose before the flash.**

**Base cooldown 22s**

![plant.gif](https://trello.com/1/cards/6b0354dad1841cedb2f6fd9a/attachments/6b03648e77b8bc81fd3e1eac/previews/6b03649077b8bc81fd3e20c0/download/plant.gif)

## Disregard

**Throw Dolly Dagger where you aim. A clean hit leaves it stuck in your opponent. Headshots deal extra damage. Get close and use Pull Free to tear it out. On a miss, the dagger turns around and cuts enemies on its way back.**

**Base cooldown 13s**

![THROW.gif](https://trello.com/1/cards/6b0354dad1841cedb2f6fd9a/attachments/6b0365035c9fc3df1db08a31/previews/6b0365065c9fc3df1db08e1a/download/THROW.gif)

## Capture Reflection

**Hold G and keep aiming at the same enemy within 30 studs for 1.5 seconds. The beam grows wider as you hold them in view. Their face stays in the blade for 15 seconds. Use Shed the Blame while you have their reflection to pass seven tenths of incoming damage to them for twelve seconds. The transfer can’t finish them off. Steadier Than Hands protects you near the planted dagger and returns attack damage to each attacker. Press Steadier Than Hands again to recall the planted dagger and end its protection.**

![CAPTURE.gif](https://trello.com/1/cards/6b0354dad1841cedb2f6fd9a/attachments/6b0365ffbffe74af48f6bb92/previews/6b036605bffe74af48f6c3d9/download/CAPTURE.gif)

## Shed the Blame (2 Bars)

**Capture a face first, then spend two EX bars. For twelve seconds the enemy in your blade takes 70% of each combat hit meant for you.**

**Base cooldown 20s**
