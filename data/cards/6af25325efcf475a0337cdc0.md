## Original description

## Lore:

**“Lucky you! You’ve got more luck than anyone in the world”**

**The Stand of Pocoloco. Unlike most Stands, Hey Ya! has no physical combat ability. Instead, it offers encouragement and advice to Pocoloco, guiding him toward incredibly lucky outcomes.**

## Whispers From Hey Ya!

**Follow Hey Ya!'s advice in time to gain luck and recover a little health.**

**Follow the advice above Hey Ya! before it expires. You may be asked to jump, pose, block, turn, or dash in a certain direction.**

**Base cooldown: 2s**
