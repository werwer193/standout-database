## Lore:

**“This is The World’s true power!”**

**The Stand of DIO. A Close-Range Power Type Stand with immense strength and speed that rivals Star Platinum. its signature ability allows it to stop time completely, allowing DIO to move freely while the universe is frozen.**

## Time Stop / Road Roller [TAP AGAIN] / Quick Time Stop [HOLD] [4 Bars]

**Tap for a 2.5 to 4 second Quick Time Stop. The 1.2-second windup holds the pose for 0.45 seconds and works with the Stand dismissed. Hold for 0.18 seconds to start full Time Stop with the Stand summoned. During the windup, you move at 25% of your base walk speed. The freeze goes through parries. You can finish the hold while taking damage over time, including fire and poison. Direct hits can interrupt the windup. Counter another stop instantly:**

- **At least 25 more Ability: overwrite it**
- **Within 24 Ability: both timers run independently**
- **At least 25 less Ability: movement ends when either timer expires An expired caster stays frozen while the other stop continues Full duration: 5 seconds at base Ability to 10 at 166 Ability**

**Base cooldown: 36s**
