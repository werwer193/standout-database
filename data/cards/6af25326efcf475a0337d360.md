## Original description

## **Lore:**

**“GUGURGUURGURUGRUGUR”**

**The stand of Carne, one of the Boss’s bodyguards. Notorious B.I.G only awakens once its user is dead, driven by the hatred Carne died with. it hunts whatever moves fastest around it and eats anything it catches, growing larger the more it consumes. Because its user is already dead, there is nothing left of him that can be killed in the usual way.**

## Death Wish

**While Notorious B.I.G’s user is alive, every hit against them does 50% more damage.**

## Hate

**Landing hits, getting hurt, Stalking and insulting people  all build Hate, up to 250. It slowly fades when you haven’t added any for a few seconds. The more Hate you die with, the more Mass Notorious B.I.G rises with, and at full Hate it rises bigger.**

## Grudge

**Anyone who hurts you builds a Grudge against you, more the harder they hit, and whoever kills you gets a full one. It shows as a red bar over their head that only you can see. The dead Notorious B.I.G deals up to 50% more damage  to enemies with a Grudge. Grudges fade over time.**

## Hateful Punches

**Notorious B.I.G throws a slow barrage of hateful punches. Every hit builds Hate, and the last one staggers. Taking a hit interrupts it.**

**Base cooldown: 14s**

![hateful.gif](https://trello.com/1/cards/6ad640de1af12f56886658a5/attachments/6adfd7da313c0203da197c76/previews/6adfd7de313c0203da1980e8/download/hateful.gif)

## Insult

**Hiss and scream insults at everyone around you. Each enemy in range takes 20% more damage for 8 seconds, gives you Hate, and loses any Grudge they had against you.**

**Base cooldown: 14s**

![insult.gif](https://trello.com/1/cards/6ad640de1af12f56886658a5/attachments/6adfd831149f4b74483601a2/previews/6adfd833149f4b744836033f/download/insult.gif)

## Tumorous Pulse

**Notorious B.I.G steps out in front of you and smashes the ground, sending a wave of tumors bursting forward through it. Anyone it catches is thrown far forward and knocked down. Blocking it keeps you on your feet.**

**Base cooldown: 12s**

![pulse.gif](https://trello.com/1/cards/6ad640de1af12f56886658a5/attachments/6adfd881d9cab3fc0e89e53e/previews/6adfd884d9cab3fc0e89eb9a/download/pulse.gif)

## Stalk (HOLD)

**Hold to stare down an enemy in front of you. While they aren’t looking at you, or you’re too far away for them to notice, you build Hate. If anyone close by looks at you, you’re spotted and the stalk ends. It also ends once your Hate is full.**

**Base cooldown: 6s**

![STALK.gif](https://trello.com/1/cards/6ad640de1af12f56886658a5/attachments/6adfd900e24c145ecefb52c1/previews/6adfd904e24c145ecefb5621/download/STALK.gif)

## Good Luck (3 Bars)

**Costs 3 EX bars and all of your Hate. Your stand is put away and you burst into Notorious B.I.G on the spot, starting with 40 extra Mass. Works even while your next transformation is on cooldown.**

**Base cooldown: 30s**

![luck.gif](https://trello.com/1/cards/6ad640de1af12f56886658a5/attachments/6adfd99999af9ad31c263acd/previews/6adfd99d99af9ad31c2643e4/download/luck.gif)

## Vengeful Life

**the moment you would start Bleeding out, Notorious B.I.G emerges and you become it in the same life. Your health becomes Mass. It drains all the time and only eating brings it back, so you have to keep feeding. When your Mass runs out, you die for  real, and your next death within a minute is a normal one.**

## Blind

**As Notorious B.I.G you only see a short way through a dark fog. Past that, anyone walking shows up as a faint pink square, and anyone running or driving shows up as a bright one from much farther away, even through walls. While you’re eating or riding someone, you see through their eyes.**

![image.webp](https://trello.com/1/cards/6ad640de1af12f56886658a5/attachments/6adfda1aa0f2cf2cbdf770e1/previews/6adfda1aa0f2cf2cbdf7715b/download/image.webp)

## Can’t swim

**As Notorious B.I.G, deep water (the sea, lakes and pools, not puddles) kills you on the spot; the body sinks to the bottom and you respawn a few seconds later. Riding a boat is safe. While you’re still alive you swim like anyone else.**

![sink.gif](https://trello.com/1/cards/6ad640de1af12f56886658a5/attachments/6adfdadf81c6d51507d26e1f/previews/6adfdae581c6d51507d27414/download/sink.gif)

## Mouthful

**As Notorious B.I.G, picking up an object swallows it instead of carrying it. Attack or press the pick-up key again to spit it back out in front of you. It can’t throw things, and cars are too big to pick up.**

![swallow.gif](https://trello.com/1/cards/6ad640de1af12f56886658a5/attachments/6adfdb99278ee85ad9434d68/previews/6adfdb9d278ee85ad9435263/download/swallow.gif)

## Consumption

**Leap forward and latch onto the first enemy or loose object you reach. Enemies are eaten for a few seconds, giving you Mass with every bite. Objects are swallowed whole. Enemies with a Grudge against you take more damage.**

**Alternate form only, while summoned.**

**Base cooldown: 10s**

![consume.gif](https://trello.com/1/cards/6ad640de1af12f56886658a5/attachments/6adfdc6e4262b57abd0481ea/previews/6adfdc744262b57abd048725/download/consume.gif)

## Symbiosis

**When Consumption kills someone, Notorious B.I.G climbs inside their body and gets it back up. The body has a quarter of its old max health, and your hunger stops while you wear it. When the body gives out, you burst back out with the Mass you had before. Use Conceal to leave it early. Only an ordinary human body can be worn; animals like seals and cats, and story or boss characters, are just eaten.**

## Starving Tendrils

**Tendrils shoot at up to three of the closest things moving around you. Each hand that lands deals solid damage and grows over their arm, causing Weakness for 15 seconds. If one lands, use it again within 3 seconds to pull yourself to the nearest one hit. Reaches farther the bigger you are.**

**Base cooldown: 12s**

![tendril.gif](https://trello.com/1/cards/6ad640de1af12f56886658a5/attachments/6adfdd2ddd81ccd0063cd6ea/previews/6adfdd2edd81ccd0063cd86a/download/tendril.gif)

**Press Q to pull yourself forward with a tendril or attach onto a loose object or a person.**

## Conceal

**While riding someone’s back, seep into their clothes and vanish completely. Use it again to come out. A heavy attack on them knocks you out of them.**

**Base cooldown: 1.5s**

![conceal.gif](https://trello.com/1/cards/6ad640de1af12f56886658a5/attachments/6adfde9de5e764c73994af91/previews/6adfde9fe5e764c73994b312/download/conceal.gif)

## Flesh Dome

**EVASIVE; works while stunned, and can’t be hit out of when used that way. Tendrils burst out in every direction around you and above Notorious B.I.G, hurting everyone close by and throwing them away from you. Reaches farther the bigger you are.**

**Base cooldown: 12s**
