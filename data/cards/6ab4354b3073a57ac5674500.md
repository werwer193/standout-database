## Original description

## Lore:

**“You’re not a normal monkey, are you?”**

**The stand of Forever. Unlike spiritual Stands, Strength is bound to a physical object; A massive freighter. It allows the user to manipulate the ship’s structure freely, weaponizing pipes, floors, and propellers with overwhelming force**.
