## Original description

## Lore:

**“The desert night is often cold… but not tonight.”**

**The Stand of Arabia Fats. It manifests as a massive, sun-like square that radiates scorching heat and intense light. It can fire high-precision lasers and increase the surrounding temperature to lethal levels.**

## Solar Flare [2 Bars]

**Release a flash of sunlight that burns and blinds nearby enemies.**

**Base cooldown: 28s**
