# There is a chance that the FSA (Food Safety Agency) shows up, if they show up they will ask to inspect your base. ([https://trello.com/c/Whb8eQEV/770-food-safety-agency-inspector](https://trello.com/c/Whb8eQEV/770-food-safety-agency-inspector "smartCard-inline"))

# If they inspect your base and see that you are growing Rokakaka, it will be seized.

# You can choose to either:

1. **Let them inspect the base**
2. **Ask for a warrant**

# Upon asking for a warrant they will call the [https://trello.com/c/ml9GjbyK/772-police](https://trello.com/c/ml9GjbyK/772-police "smartCard-inline")  and give you a search warrant.

# If you attack them during the inspection they will call the [https://trello.com/c/ml9GjbyK/772-police](https://trello.com/c/ml9GjbyK/772-police "smartCard-inline")  and [https://trello.com/c/KKLUqQOa/771-swat](https://trello.com/c/KKLUqQOa/771-swat "smartCard-inline")
