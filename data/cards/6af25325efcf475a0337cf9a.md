## Integrated Stand

**Press E to activate Boiling Water, You can still use your usual weapon attacks.**

## Heat

**Heat rises while the Stand is active and when you use its boiling attacks. Above 70 Heat, you lose health each second. The hotter you get, the more health you lose. Hold Let Off Steam to cool down. Cold or dismissing the stand Stand also lowers Heat. At full Heat, your light attacks set enemies on fire.**

![image.webp](https://trello.com/1/cards/6ab7e2f73f2f794f97be5184/attachments/6ab7e580cb1614f8ca325943/previews/6ab7e581cb1614f8ca32599b/download/image.webp)

## Bring to a Boil

**Boil the water you’re aiming at, including any puddles touching it. The steam repeatedly damages and stuns enemies. Boiling a soaked enemy burns them repeatedly for 6 seconds.**

**Hold to highlight water and targets. Cold protects against this move.**

**Base cooldown: 8s**

## Super Soaker

**Fire four arcing shots of water, Each hit leaves a puddle, and enemies hit directly become Soaked, blocking stops the damage and keeps them dry.**

**Base cooldown: 5s**

![soaker.gif](https://trello.com/1/cards/6ab7e2f73f2f794f97be5184/attachments/6ab7e90ac352c174e568ad68/previews/6ab7e90bc352c174e568ae1b/download/soaker.gif)

## Hot Potato

**Throw a water balloon that bursts on impact, soaking nearby enemies and leaving a large puddle. Hitting someone directly also knocks them back and briefly stuns them.**

**Base cooldown: 8s**

![throw.gif](https://trello.com/1/cards/6ab7e2f73f2f794f97be5184/attachments/6ab7e92af21ab13e357c4b92/previews/6ab7e92bf21ab13e357c4c05/download/throw.gif)

## Let Off Steam / Vent [HOLD]

**Hold to cool yourself with a growing cloud of steam. Nearby enemies take damage and are stunned. You can move slowly while venting. Let go, cool off completely or take a hit to stop.**

**Base cooldown: 14s**

# EX [Awakening Moves]

## Flash Boil [3 Bars]

**Heat the water on a Soaked enemy, then make it burst. If the hit leaves them below 15% health, they explode. Cold protects them, and taking cover or getting out of range stops the move. Adds 25 Heat on hit.**

**Base cooldown: 24s**

![flasboil.gif](https://trello.com/1/cards/6ab7e2f73f2f794f97be5184/attachments/6ab7eade8a8665b1283dfc88/previews/6ab7eae18a8665b1283e0076/download/flasboil.gif)

## Pressure Cooker [2 Bars]

**Build pressure in a fire hydrant or your flying water balloon until it bursts, launching nearby enemies. Hydrants leave scalding water for five seconds. Cold protects against the scalding water. Adds 20 Heat.**

**Base cooldown: 22s**

## Boil Dry / Find Water [HOLD]

**Boil away a puddle and any puddles touching it. As the water shrinks, the steam rapidly damages enemies and briefly stuns them. Water shots passing through it evaporate. Hold to highlight water. Adds 15 Heat.**
