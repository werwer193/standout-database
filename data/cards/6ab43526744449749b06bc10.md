## Original description

## Lore:

**“Glory Days.”**

**The Stand of Bobby Jean. It is a pure ability Stand that allows Bobby Jean to manipulate the speed of the bullets he fires.**

### Gun Controls

**Use E to draw the gun and M1 to fire. The magazine must be refilled with Reload. Glory changes the behavior of newly fired rounds, while Elevator Trap sets up a seperate crossfire.**

## M1 Light Combo & M2 Attack

## Glory

**Toggle Glory to suspend newly fired rounds in midair.**

**Base cooldown: 0.3s**

![glory2.gif](https://trello.com/1/cards/6ab43526744449749b06bc10/attachments/6ab54f4a96c320fe7a27b965/previews/6ab54f5196c320fe7a27c17b/download/glory2.gif)

## Knock Knock

**Kick forward, striking enemies in front of you.**

**Base cooldown: 28s**

![knockknock.gif](https://trello.com/1/cards/6ab43526744449749b06bc10/attachments/6ab55020e7bcfb7165e55a77/previews/6ab55023e7bcfb7165e568e2/download/knockknock.gif)

## Elevator Trap

**Set a hidden crossfire with Glory Days. The bullets close in when an enemy enters.**

**Base cooldown: 23s**

![trapbullet.gif](https://trello.com/1/cards/6ab43526744449749b06bc10/attachments/6ab5509cf6d8c1165bf6fc0c/previews/6ab550a0f6d8c1165bf6fefb/download/trapbullet.gif)
