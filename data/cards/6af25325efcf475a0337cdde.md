## Original description

## **Lore:**

## **“Reality is the lifeblood of entertainment.”**

**The Stand of Rohan Kishibe, encountered in Hawaii. While physically weak, it posesses the terrifying ability to turn people into books. By reading them, Rohan can learn their secrets, and by writing in them, he can force them to obey absolute commands.**

## Book Transmutation

**Turn an enemy’s face into a book for 1.5-2 seconds based on Ability, allowing blocks but not perfect blocks and closing when either participant is hit.**

**Ability Scaling: At 166 Ability; 17.5 stud range and 2 second book, with damage scaling 80% Ability / 20% Strength.**

**Base cooldown: 16s**

## Written Command

**Write a command on a book target, with Ability extending Forget Motion from 2 to 2.5 seconds, Sleep from 2 to 3, Forget Me from 6 to 10 and Lower Your Guard from 8 to 10, while Fly Away keeps its 4x wall-splat multiplier.**

**Ability Scaling: At 166 Ability: 25-stud range, Forget Motion 2.5s, Sleep 3s, Forget me 10s, Lower Your Guard 10s, and Fly Away force 120.**

**Base cooldown: 15s**

## Page Rip

**Tear a page from a grabbed book target for 16 base damage.**

**Ability Scaling: Damage scales 70% Strength / 30% Ability, with 4-6 seconds of knockback vulnerability at an unchanged 1.45x multiplier.**

**Base cooldown: 28s**

![rip.gif](https://trello.com/1/cards/6ab43569c8e358d66b0917ae/attachments/6ab566a4d4308ada7da5d089/previews/6ab566abd4308ada7da5d964/download/rip.gif)

## Safety Lock

**Write on an enemy’s open face to refuse their next two direct attacks against you within 15 seconds.**

**Base cooldown: 34s**

# EX [Awakening Moves]

## Manga [3 Bars]

**Hold up Heaven’s Door’s manga panel to do 15 base damage and turn nearby viewers into books for 3-3.5 seconds based on Ability,  without allowing perfect blocks.**

**Ability Scaling: 80% Ability / 20% Strength damage; 32-40 stud radius; 3-3.5 second books.**

**Base cooldown: 45s**

![manga.gif](https://trello.com/1/cards/6ab43569c8e358d66b0917ae/attachments/6ab56731feca3f07c92c55fc/previews/6ab56735feca3f07c92c594d/download/manga.gif)

## Become My Character [4 Bars]

**Command someone you’ve turned into a book to fight for you with their Stand.**

**Ability Scaling: At 166 Ability: control rises from 12s to 20 seconds, application range from 15 to 22 studs and guard detection from 65 to 85 studs.**

**Base cooldown: 120s**

![character.gif](https://trello.com/1/cards/6ab43569c8e358d66b0917ae/attachments/6ab568caaf8dbfe98df0ed2f/previews/6ab568ebaf8dbfe98df1215f/download/character.gif)

## **Chapter Counter [3 Bars]**

**Spend 2 EX bars to counter an attack and book the attacker for 3.5-4 seconds based on Ability, with stun ending alongside ragdoll.**

**Ability Scaling: Book duration scales from 3-4 seconds by 166 Ability without changing the counter activation window.**

**Base cooldown: 35s**

![counter.gif](https://trello.com/1/cards/6ab43569c8e358d66b0917ae/attachments/6ab568f9498d6425c3cfee0e/previews/6ab56902498d6425c3d004d8/download/counter.gif)
