## Original description

## Lore:

**“The moral of the story is that miracles take dedication.”**

**Aya Tsuji runs Morioh’s Cinderella salon, where she uses her Stand to change people’s faces and bodies. She wants to help her clients find love, and her work can change their luck as well as their looks. The results can depend on following her instructions. Her ability to swap faces can also help someone hide who they really are.**

## Faces and fate

**Choose one of five faces with Enhancement Selector, then apply it with Change of Face; hold to treat yourself. Miracle lasts until death. Botching the treatment on yourself or a non-user gives Misfortune for 60 seconds, up to 90 with Ability, and Ability makes the treatment up to 30% faster. Special Lipstick cures misfortune you caused; hold it to sell cures for 100 coins.**
