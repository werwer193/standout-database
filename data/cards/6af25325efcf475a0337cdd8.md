# Lore:

# **“I, Diego Brando, shall seize the corpse!”**

**The Stand of Diego Brando from Another Universe. A Close-Range Power Type with terrifying precision. it possesses the ability to stop time, allowing Diego to unleash a volley of knives while his opponents are frozen in place.**

**Unleash a rapid flurry of punches on your opponent.**

![barragetw.gif](https://trello.com/1/cards/6ab4355eec7c0220af83d519/attachments/6ab57dd1c757397397375592/previews/6ab57ddfc7573973973776e4/download/barragetw.gif)

## Knife Throw

**Tap to throw two volleys toward your cursor, starting with four knives per volley. You can throw with your Stand dismissed. Each knife launches at 247.5 studs per second and slows to a stop along a quadratic ease-out curve. Hold for 0.18 seconds during stopped time to circle a nearby target and throw one knife at a time. Each orbit knife hangs a few studs from its launch point. When time resumes, it flies toward where the target was when you threw it. Both throws reach 162.5 studs at 1 Ability and 243.75 at 166.**

**Base cooldown: 25s**

![knifes.gif](https://trello.com/1/cards/6af25325efcf475a0337cdd8/attachments/6af8a8ca464d8fbc12d28a1f/previews/6af8a8cd464d8fbc12d28cea/download/knifes.gif)

## Donut

**Punch a hole clean through your opponent's guard. A perfect block or a well-timed Tense Up stops the blink in front of the defender. If Time Stop catches your windup, the forward blink resumes when time moves again. Starting the move during stopped time or while piloting punches in place.**

**Base cooldown: 30s**

## Arsenal

## Deadly Shots

**Fire a heavy round for 1 EX. Tap again for a Stand follow-up costing 1 more EX. Both hits break guard and can be perfect blocked. A landed follow-up causes Concussion. During your own Time Stop, hold with your Stand out to shoot a visible target at any distance. The shot resumes time and the Stand punches them to your left for 1 EX total.**

**ABILITY SCALING: Damage scales 40% with Strength and 60% with Ability.**

**Base cooldown: 7s**

## Time Stop (4 Bars) / Quick Time Stop (3 Bars)

**Tap for a 2.5 to 4 second Quick Time Stop. The 1.2-second windup holds the pose for 0.45 seconds and works with the Stand dismissed. Hold for 0.18 seconds to start full Time Stop with the Stand summoned. During the windup, you move at 25% of your base walk speed. The freeze goes through parries. You can finish the hold while taking damage over time, including fire and poison. Direct hits can interrupt the windup. Counter another stop instantly:**

- **At least 25 more Ability: overwrite it**
- **Within 24 Ability: both timers run independently**
- **At least 25 less Ability: movement ends when either timer expires An expired caster stays frozen while the other stop continues Full duration: 5 seconds at base Ability to 10 at 166 Ability**

**Base cooldown: 22s**

![twauts.gif](https://trello.com/1/cards/6ab4355eec7c0220af83d519/attachments/6ab57f7094a10781befe163b/previews/6ab57f7f94a10781befe303b/download/twauts.gif)
