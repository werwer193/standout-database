## Original description

## **Lore:**

**“They’re my friends… my precious Harvest!”**

**The stand of Shigekiyo “Shigechi” Yangu. A colony of roughly 500 tiny insect-like units acting as a single will. Each unit is feeble,  But the swarm can scour a entire town for anything of value and overwhelm enemies through sheer numbers.**

## **Colony Carry [TAP AGAIN TO STOP]**

**The colony scoops you up and hauls you rapidly across any surface (including walls). Tap again to stop early.**

**Base cooldown: 25s**

![carry2.gif](https://trello.com/1/cards/6ab4352ea6528c9c7a197e4a/attachments/6ab4ea0c9343c511212dff9e/previews/6ab4ea3c9343c511212e5b90/download/carry2.gif)

## **Harvest Ambush/RECALL [HOLD]**

**Tap to plant a Harvest squad on a floor, wall, or ceiling. They ambush enemies who come near. Hold to recall your squads. Active ambush squads must be recalled or spent before a normal unsummon. (Procs Poison)**

**Base cooldown: 0.45s**

# EX [Awakening Moves]

## Poured Into Veins [3 Bars]

**“I poured it into your veins!” All nine Harvest combine into one concealed trap and overwhelm its victim with poisoned stings.**

**Base cooldown: 16s**

## Snatch [2 Bars]

**Send Harvest to take a consumable from an enemy’s hand and bring it back. Getting hit on the way interrupts them and drops anything they took.**

**Base cooldown: 18s**
