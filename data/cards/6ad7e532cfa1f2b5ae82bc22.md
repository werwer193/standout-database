## Original description

## Lore:

**“That was a mean thing to do… Treating me like an object… not that I mind”**

**The Stand of D an G. Yo-Yo Ma follows people and offers to help them, hiding its corrosive saliva behind a friendly face. Its tough body can be knocked around without hurting it. It uses mosquitos and water to spread saliva whilst nobody is looking.**

## Stand Mechanics:

## A Devil in Disguise/Automatic Stand

**Hold E while aiming at someone within 100 studs of the stand to follow them. It follows from behind and stops when watched. Yo-Yo Ma sends mosquitos after its chosen target, Acidic Saliva leaves them Muted.**

![2026-10-2712-29-29-ezgif.com-video-to-gif-converter.gif](https://trello.com/1/cards/6ad7e532cfa1f2b5ae82bc22/attachments/6ae09dd34c767d1f89e553ae/download/2026-10-2712-29-29-ezgif.com-video-to-gif-converter.gif)

## Repulsive

**Yo-Yo Ma takes no damage, but can be stunned or knocked away. Close hits splash saliva onto the attacker and makes them Stinky. Sending it flying splashes nearby enemies too.**

![2026-10-2712-30-38-ezgif.com-video-to-gif-converter.gif](https://trello.com/1/cards/6ad7e532cfa1f2b5ae82bc22/attachments/6ae09ef06b098d1db6700f81/download/2026-10-2712-30-38-ezgif.com-video-to-gif-converter.gif)

## Crybaby

**If the user is attacked while piloting the stand, they will cry out and be stunned for a few seconds.**

![2026-10-2712-24-31-ezgif.com-video-to-gif-converter.gif](https://trello.com/1/cards/6ad7e532cfa1f2b5ae82bc22/attachments/6ae09fe24a7846e40b45cc76/download/2026-10-2712-24-31-ezgif.com-video-to-gif-converter.gif)

## An Expert Craftsman

**Talk to Yo-Yo Ma while it is parked or following someone else. It can craft using your materials, heal 30 health, or shine your boots to grant Encouraged, giving 10% more damage for 40 seconds. Collect finished crafts within five seconds before someone else does. Your materials are only spent when the item is collected.**

![2026-10-2712-32-11-ezgif.com-video-to-gif-converter.gif](https://trello.com/1/cards/6ad7e532cfa1f2b5ae82bc22/attachments/6ae0a1042979b87119c744f9/download/2026-10-2712-32-11-ezgif.com-video-to-gif-converter.gif)

## Tainted Item

**Hold to cycle through items, then tap to make one in Yo-Yo Ma’s empty stomach. Use Treacherous Hoarder to spit it out. Real Mysterious Arrows and Rokakas take 60 to 30 minutes to make again. SSSS Rokakas and Over Heaven Arrows are fakes that deal delayed acid damage when used.**

**Base cooldown: 15s**

## Drooling Menace

**Coat the ground and nearby objects in Acidic Saliva, damaging enemies and leaving them Muted. It also contaminates connected water.**

**ABILITY SCALING: Ability makes the pool up to 30% wider.**

**Base cooldown: 12s**

![2026-10-2620-42-58-ezgif.com-video-to-gif-converter.gif](https://trello.com/1/cards/6ad7e532cfa1f2b5ae82bc22/attachments/6adfbbb672b6efad2b0070d3/download/2026-10-2620-42-58-ezgif.com-video-to-gif-converter.gif)

## Treacherous Hoarder

**Swallow nearby items to carry them back to you, then interact to collect them. Use the move again to spit out items or launch swallowed throwables, leaving a trail of saliva. Heavy hits force them out.**

**Base cooldown: 3s**

## EX: [Awakening Moveset]

## Impromptu Camoflauge

**Make a bush and interact with it to hide inside. You become nearly invisible but walk slower. Attacking, sprinting, dashing, or getting hit reveals you.**

**Costs 1 EX Bar**

**Base cooldown: 25s**

![2026-10-2711-07-17-ezgif.com-video-to-gif-converter.gif](https://trello.com/1/cards/6ad7e532cfa1f2b5ae82bc22/attachments/6ae0862d89276cbc739b1239/download/2026-10-2711-07-17-ezgif.com-video-to-gif-converter.gif)

## Pestilent Trap

**Hide mosquitoes in a throwable. Only you can see them before an enemy touches it and triggers the swarm. The swarm spreads acidic saliva and briefly staggers them. Hitting the object disarms it. You can set two traps.**

**Costs 1 EX Bar**

**Base cooldown: 10s**

![2026-10-2711-14-05-ezgif.com-video-to-gif-converter.gif](https://trello.com/1/cards/6ad7e532cfa1f2b5ae82bc22/attachments/6ae089f246f9145ad229a2ed/download/2026-10-2711-14-05-ezgif.com-video-to-gif-converter.gif)

## Hey, What’s That?

**Make nearby enemies watching Yo-Yo Ma turn to look where it points, giving its mosquitos a chance to attack.**

**Costs 1 EX Bar**

**Base cooldown: 18s**

![2026-10-2712-22-51-ezgif.com-video-to-gif-converter.gif](https://trello.com/1/cards/6ad7e532cfa1f2b5ae82bc22/attachments/6ae09b61935776976cd52a1e/download/2026-10-2712-22-51-ezgif.com-video-to-gif-converter.gif)

## Gourmand Of Spirits

**Swallow an enemy’s stand for 10 seconds, stopping them from using it. They can still fight without their stand. A heavy hit on Yo-Yo Ma frees it early.**

**Costs 2 EX Bars**

**Base cooldown: 35s**
