# Glasses

**Use E to put on your glasses. Wear them to see Stress and use Target, Fear Sense, Locate and Yes Or No? Taking them off clears your target and ends Locate and Fear Sense. Fury and Stress Test still work without them.**

![equip.gif](https://trello.com/1/cards/6af25326efcf475a0337d32a/attachments/6af4cd988079f1c9b4ee87f5/previews/6af4cda08079f1c9b4ee9378/download/equip.gif)

## Piling Nematodes

**Attacks and Stress, up to 20 stacks. Each stack gives the next damaging skill 3% more damage, up to 60%, or 5% per stack for a** [https://trello.com/c/wJ9sofSB/196-200-balloons](https://trello.com/c/wJ9sofSB/196-200-balloons "smartCard-inline") **skill. That skill spends the Stress and keeps the bonus for all its hits. Those hits do not add Stress. After 15 seconds without another hit, Stress loses one stack every 0.6 seconds.**

![lyin.gif](https://trello.com/1/cards/6af25326efcf475a0337d32a/attachments/6af4ce73da5f2fc3f831ffa6/previews/6af4ce85da5f2fc3f8323724/download/lyin.gif)

## Yes Or No?

**While wearing your glasses, give a nearby player 5 seconds to answer a yes-or-no question, about themselves or anything at all. You can keep fighting while they answer. A correct answer adds 3 Stress. A wrong answer or no answer makes you shoot their leg, spending one Fury round and leaving them Crippled for 9 seconds. They can answer correctly, interrupt you, break sight or move out of range to avoid the shot.**

**Base cooldown: 25s**

## Fear Sense

**See an enemy’s EX. Choosing your locked target or the nearest enemy. Drains 1 Stress every 3 seconds and ends when they run out. Use again to stop.**

**Base cooldown: 1s**

![sense.gif](https://trello.com/1/cards/6af25326efcf475a0337d32a/attachments/6af4d0116f114c818d8f5940/previews/6af4d0186f114c818d8f5f56/download/sense.gif)

## **Fury**

**Draw your 10-round pistol and fire with light attacks. You cannot sprint while it is drawn. Use again to holster and keep your remaining ammo. Hold to reload. Drawing an empty pistol also reloads it.**

**Base cooldown: 0.35s**

## Stress Test

**Hold an enemy at gunpoint for up to 4 seconds. You can walk with them, and they take the hits coming from in front of you instead of you. A hit from behind makes you let them go. Click to shoot if they have less EX than you, spending one Fury round. Otherwise you choke them and drop them. Running out of time or ammo also makes you choke them.**

**Base cooldown: 24s**
