# "You survived that? Congratulations!"

**Keicho Nijimura commands Bad Company, a miniature army of riflemen, Green Berets, tanks and attack helicopters. Their weapons fire live rounds.**

## Military Might

**You start with three Infantry units, one Green Beret unit, one tank unit and one helicopter unit. That uses all 14 Military Might. Infantry and Green Berets cost 2 per unit. Tanks and helicopters cost 3.**

**Send a unit home to free its Might immediately. If an enemy destroys it, its Might stays spent until the replacement arrives 25 seconds later.**

![image.webp](https://trello.com/1/cards/6af8b2c29ba5f50e86a52b13/attachments/6b0359f1e9cebb3b59ed75fd/previews/6b0359f1e9cebb3b59ed78dc/download/image.webp)

## Unit Type

**Tap G to cycle through the unit types you have out. The type you select deals 25% more damage. Hold G to select everyone. This lets you move or recall the whole army at once, but no unit gets the damage bonus.**

![switchs.gif](https://trello.com/1/cards/6af8b2c29ba5f50e86a52b13/attachments/6b035ababecec5d6281ea4b4/previews/6b035abfbecec5d6281ea8c5/download/switchs.gif)

## Troops

**Infantry fire rifles. Tanks take half damage from projectiles and have to turn their turrets before they can fire. Helicopters alternate between gunfire and missiles, but take extra damage from projectiles.**

**Green Berets hide while you pilot them. Get within 10 studs of an enemy and they leap on, stabbing for up to four seconds. The ambush uses up the unit.**

## Pilot Mode

**Press Ctrl to lead the selected units. Select helicopters to fly. With every type selected, you can lead up to three units at once. Aim at an enemy and press M1 to order the units you’re piloting to attack. Focus Fire directs the rest of the army.**

![pilooot.gif](https://trello.com/1/cards/6af8b2c29ba5f50e86a52b13/attachments/6b035b2fcc4d86710e8a6b60/previews/6b035b41cc4d86710e8a817d/download/pilooot.gif)

## Focus Fire

**Mark an enemy for six seconds. Your riflemen, tanks and helicopters open fire as soon as the target comes within reach of their weapons. Use it again to switch targets.**

**Damage scales 100% with Ability.**

**Base cooldown 10s**

![FOCUSSS.gif](https://trello.com/1/cards/6af8b2c29ba5f50e86a52b13/attachments/6b035b69af936f691608b94e/previews/6b035b80af936f691608d46f/download/FOCUSSS.gif)

## **Maneuver**

**Tap to send the selected units to hold a spot up to 180 studs away. Hold to call back the selected units that are holding a spot or flanking. You can leave your tanks behind and send your riflemen ahead. Units you're piloting stay with you.**

**Base cooldown 1s**

![maneuveeeer.gif](https://trello.com/1/cards/6af8b2c29ba5f50e86a52b13/attachments/6b035c9c05978916dddc32c8/previews/6b035ca205978916dddc3b06/download/maneuveeeer.gif)

## Flanking

**Select one unit type and use it on an enemy. Those units circle the enemy at close range until you use it again. They only fire when you use Focus Fire, or click while you pilot them. Piloting a type that is flanking puts your camera on it and it keeps circling. It doesn't work with every type selected.**

**Damage gains half the usual Ability bonus.**

**Base cooldown 3s**

![flaank.gif](https://trello.com/1/cards/6af8b2c29ba5f50e86a52b13/attachments/6b05ba1774381c73b160cf0c/previews/6b05ba2474381c73b160e249/download/flaank.gif)

## Landmine

**Set a landmine a step in front of you, or under the unit you're piloting. It arms after a moment and goes off when an enemy steps on it, throwing everyone nearby away and knocking them down. You have three charges. A mine that goes off gives its charge back after 22 seconds, and one that runs out after a second.**

**Damage scales 100% with Ability.**

**Base cooldown 2s**

## Full Scale Warfare (3 Bars)

**Call in reinforcements to fill every empty slot in the army. Units holding a position fall back to you. The reinforcements leave after six seconds.**

**Base cooldown 48s**

![fuuul.gif](https://trello.com/1/cards/6af8b2c29ba5f50e86a52b13/attachments/6b035d0563d562d357750c95/previews/6b035d0e63d562d357751884/download/fuuul.gif)

## Atomic Strike (4 Bars)

**Launch a bomb from a silo in front of you. Once it’s in the air, you have three seconds to aim. When the marker stops moving, the bomb drops and hits enemies within 14 studs of it. Enemies damaged by the blast catch fire.**

**Damage scales 100% with Ability.**

**Base cooldown 75s**

![atomic.gif](https://trello.com/1/cards/6af8b2c29ba5f50e86a52b13/attachments/6b035d75503ea75868614067/previews/6b035d83503ea75868614bfe/download/atomic.gif)

## Airstrike (2 Bars)

**A jet dives in from behind you and flies to where you aim, within your pilot range, dropping three missiles in a row. Each blast breaks guard but can be perfect blocked.**

**Damage scales 100% with Ability.**

**Base cooldown 32s**

![AIRSTRIKE.gif](https://trello.com/1/cards/6af8b2c29ba5f50e86a52b13/attachments/6b05bbee711bcd7f00f95bf4/previews/6b05bbf8711bcd7f00f96565/download/AIRSTRIKE.gif)

## Paratroopers (1 Bar)

**Drop a unit of the selected type where you aim, up to 180 studs away. Infantry and Green Berets come down by parachute, tanks under a bigger one, and helicopters fly down. With every type selected it drops Infantry. You need an open slot for that type and the Military Might it costs.**

**Base cooldown 21s**

![paratroopers.gif](https://trello.com/1/cards/6af8b2c29ba5f50e86a52b13/attachments/6b05bceebf0065bc161bc053/previews/6b05bcf3bf0065bc161bc9ab/download/paratroopers.gif)
