## Infused Throwables

**Pick up an object with your character to infuse it with Hamon. Infused heavy objects and spears release a Hamon ripple when they land. Gasoline carries Hamon into its puddle. Picking an object up through your Stand does not apply this infusion.**

## Sunlight Yellow Overdrive

**After a short windup, strike repeatedly in front of you. You move slowly  and cannot jump during the attack. The barrage wears down a blocking target’s posture and grants armor while the punches are active.**

**Base cooldown: 23s**

![yellowoverdrive.gif](https://trello.com/1/cards/6ab432a16505cfdb76c223bf/attachments/6ab587e3f47fc6b7249ff78b/previews/6ab587e7f47fc6b7249ffbfb/download/yellowoverdrive.gif)

## Scarlet Overdrive

**Wind up a close-range uppercut. An unblocked hit ignites the target and sends them upward and forward. Blocking takes posture damage, and the attack can be parried. It can also ignite nearby gasoline.**

**Base cooldown: 18s**

![scarlet2.gif](https://trello.com/1/cards/6ab432a16505cfdb76c223bf/attachments/6ab588e1891c6550642ff7f5/previews/6ab588e4891c6550642ffba4/download/scarlet2.gif)

## Zoom Punch

**Strike the nearest enemy in front of you within reach, briefly stunning them and knocking them back. The punch can be blocked or parried and cannot reach through walls. Throwing it costs 2 health, but cannot reduce you below 1 health.**

**Base cooldown: 12s**

![zoom.gif](https://trello.com/1/cards/6ab432a16505cfdb76c223bf/attachments/6abbabacc3404becd09c6475/previews/6abbabb0c3404becd09c674b/download/zoom.gif)

## Hamonic Breath

**While Hamonic lasts, you recover health, dash farther and infuse light attacks with Hamon, A fuller bar gives stronger healing and dashes. Breathe again to top it up. At full charge, the bar waits three seconds before draining. Taking a hit stops your breath, but keeps the Hamonic you already built. Effects that prevent healing still apply.**

**Base cooldown: 1s**

![breath.gif](https://trello.com/1/cards/6ab432a16505cfdb76c223bf/attachments/6abbabc210957c67af7632c5/previews/6abbabc710957c67af76443e/download/breath.gif)

## EX [Awakening Moves]

## Life-Giving Overdrive [2 Bars]

**Aim at a growing crop nearby. It grows sixty times faster for ten seconds, stopping when it is ready to harvest.**

**Base cooldown: 25s**

![lifegiving.gif](https://trello.com/1/cards/6ab432a16505cfdb76c223bf/attachments/6abbac7ab1822be1ab65c928/previews/6abbac81b1822be1ab65d8d5/download/lifegiving.gif)

## Sendo Ripple [1 Bar]

**Release a burst of Hamon, even while stunned. Repeated hits build Ripple, then the final hit knocks enemies away. The burst also deflects thrown objects. It starts twice as fast when you aren't stunned.**

**Base cooldown: 18s**

![sendoo.gif](https://trello.com/1/cards/6ab432a16505cfdb76c223bf/attachments/6ac37efd87808845bcc5efd3/previews/6ac37f0087808845bcc5f0ba/download/sendoo.gif)

## Hamon Leap [1 Bar]

**Only available with Hamon and no Stand. Start from the ground to clear a gap or close distance.**

**Base cooldown: 8s**
