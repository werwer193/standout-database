## Original description

## Lore:

**“I will pierce you through!”**

**The Stand of Hato Higashikata. A Stand integrated into the user’s footwear that allows for the hardening and elongation of the heels. These spikes possess immense piercing power, capable of penetrating stone and allowing the user to anchor themselves to walls and ceilings.**

## Ground Impale

**Target an opponent near your aim to raise a ground spike and impale them. Aims within 14 studs of the cursor; if no target is found, chooses the closest opponent within 10 studs of you. The strike position is fixed when activated, so opponents can jump or dash away during startup. Breaks guards, but can be perfect blocked by facing the incoming spike.**

**Base cooldown: 12s**

![impales.gif](https://trello.com/1/cards/6ab435404fdc1f886a54eb74/attachments/6acabdab5b318f6b91d7da4e/previews/6acabdae5b318f6b91d7dc82/download/impales.gif)
