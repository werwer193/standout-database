**Obtainment:**

**‘Burning’** _can be_ obtained from consuming the Chili Pepper item.

The Chili Pepper item is grown from the ‘Chili Pepper Seed’ purchasable from [https://trello.com/c/ji4Hw59y](https://trello.com/c/ji4Hw59y "smartCard-inline")  & grown by  [https://trello.com/c/naSGoTSr](https://trello.com/c/naSGoTSr "smartCard-inline") .

**‘Burning’** can also be obtained via fire-related stands such as Magician’s Red.
