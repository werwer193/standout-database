## Original description

## Lore:

## **“i wish it worked with the things i want though.”**

**The Stand of Usagi Alohaoe. THE MATTEKUDASAI is a small mechanical bird that hops along the ground and turns into a copy of whatever someone else needs. It needs someone else’s request before it can make a copy.**

---

## Big Jump

**The bird can make a second leap before landing. Use it to clear gaps and reach higher ground.**

![jump.gif](https://trello.com/1/cards/6af25326efcf475a0337d5d0/attachments/6af473bccda0124853f62bba/previews/6af473c0cda0124853f63832/download/jump.gif)

## False Structure

**Scan a prop, then turn the Stand into a copy. Hold to switch between learned props. Pilot the copy to move and swing it, or stop piloting to leave it in place. Tap again to recall it.**

**Base cooldown: 11s**

## Recall

**False Structure, The Gift, Weapon Wish, Not Licensed and Fake Camera can be recalled with another tap. The net and wire fence stay until they finish or break. A form’s cooldown starts when it returns**.

![recall.gif](https://trello.com/1/cards/6af25326efcf475a0337d5d0/attachments/6af475e56264f78402404077/previews/6af475e66264f784024041f9/download/recall.gif)

## Net

**Throw the stand as a net to slam and pin an enemy. Close catches hold them longer. Hold to stretch it into a wire fence that knocks enemies back. Both bypass blocking, can be parried, and can be broken.**

**Base cooldown: 14s**

![net.gif](https://trello.com/1/cards/6af25326efcf475a0337d5d0/attachments/6af472d485f6fcf4ca571e68/previews/6af472d785f6fcf4ca572386/download/net.gif)

## The Gift

**Offer a gift for another player to choose, or answer an NPC’s request. Tap again to recall it safely. Hold while an enemy carries it to make it stab or bonk them; the hit can’t be blocked or dodged.**

**Base cooldown: 17s**

![gift.gif](https://trello.com/1/cards/6af25326efcf475a0337d5d0/attachments/6af481616edb32bd069fc81b/previews/6af481656edb32bd069fce3c/download/gift.gif)

# EX Moves [Awakening Moves]

## Not Licensed [2 Bars]

**Turn the Stand into a tougher, faster sports car you or an ally can drive. Pilot it to steer slowly without sitting inside. Damage to the car hurts you too. Tap again to turn it back.**

**Base cooldown: 38s**

![carr.gif](https://trello.com/1/cards/6af25326efcf475a0337d5d0/attachments/6af47561cea87fc664f0c7da/previews/6af47576cea87fc664f0e534/download/carr.gif)

## Fake Camera [2 Bars]

**Turn the Stand into a wall or ceiling camera. Crouch to see its sweep. Enemies it spots draw nearby NPCs toward them; spotting you or an ally makes pursuers lose interest. One hit breaks the camera and hurts you.**

**Base cooldown: 31s**

![camera.gif](https://trello.com/1/cards/6af25326efcf475a0337d5d0/attachments/6af4765a500f00c12c88828f/previews/6af4765f500f00c12c888f06/download/camera.gif)
