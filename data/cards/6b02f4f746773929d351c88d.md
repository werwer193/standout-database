## **Cry For Help**

**Draw nearby opponents' attention toward the host.**

**Base cooldown: 12s**

![heklp.gif](https://trello.com/1/cards/6b02f4f746773929d351c88d/attachments/6b03590e820a03c121d82e8b/previews/6b035911820a03c121d83317/download/heklp.gif)

## Insult!

**Gain 15% of your EX meter. Provoke nearby enemy NPCs and make enemy players rush the host for one light attack.**

**Base cooldown: 10s**

![taunt.gif](https://trello.com/1/cards/6b02f4f746773929d351c88d/attachments/6b0358a0fc492b4512f5dd81/previews/6b0358a3fc492b4512f5e3bf/download/taunt.gif)

## Taunt

**Lower the host's damage or resistance by 15% for five seconds.**

**Base cooldown: 12s**

![insult.gif](https://trello.com/1/cards/6b02f4f746773929d351c88d/attachments/6b03585ebbb7ae4f4ac40c4d/previews/6b035863bbb7ae4f4ac416f4/download/insult.gif)

## SCREAM!

**Stun the current host for the scream animation and deafen them for ten seconds.**

**Base cooldown: 20s**
