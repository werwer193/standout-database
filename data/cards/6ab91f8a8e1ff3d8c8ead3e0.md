## Original description

## **Lore:**

**“Your sentence to torture is now repealed! Now! immediately! You are to be executed!”**

**The Stand of Mike O., President Funny Valentine’s bodyguard. Tubular Bells lets him inflate metal into balloon animals that pursue a memorized scent. He can reshape them from a distance, and their elastic bodies squeeze through narrow openings. When a balloon bursts, it returns to its original metal form: a nail hidden in a animal or a heavy sheet raised overhead can become a deadly trap.**

## Five Animals

**Nails and gas cans make dogs, hydrants make swans, trash cans make turtles, signs make snakes, and metal benches or empty cars make lions. Holding Inflate longer increases size, damage and knockback.**

## Balloon Control

**Tap Ctrl to cycle through your balloon animals and back to your body. Move toward your camera; aim up or down to change height. Hold Ctrl to return immediately.**

![pilots.gif](https://trello.com/1/cards/6ab91f8a8e1ff3d8c8ead3e0/attachments/6aba76fa0af563582a393359/previews/6aba76fe0af563582a393908/download/pilots.gif)

## Early Pop

**Press E while piloting to pop the balloon and drop its original object.**

![carssss.gif](https://trello.com/1/cards/6ab91f8a8e1ff3d8c8ead3e0/attachments/6aba77a7e40ff8b80f1aa2f3/previews/6aba77a9e40ff8b80f1aa9b7/download/carssss.gif)

## Inflate Balloon

**Hold while aiming at a metal object to blow it into a balloon animal. Blow longer to make it bigger and hit harder. Tap to send an animal forward; it returns if it misses. Keep up to five, including duplicates. (Cursor over object)**

## Pick Up Scent

**Hold while aiming at a throwable to collect the scent of its last owner, or someone nearby. Tap to send your animals after that person, even through walls. Tap again to recall them. The scent lasts 45 seconds.**

![scent.gif](https://trello.com/1/cards/6ab91f8a8e1ff3d8c8ead3e0/attachments/6aba7ad9d0eb355920e023d1/previews/6aba7adfd0eb355920e02a31/download/scent.gif)

## Balloon Flower

**Plant a flower in front of you. It takes one second to grow, then blasts enemies who approach from the front. The burst knocks them down; enemies behind it are safe. Keep up to three flowers, each lasting 25 seconds.**

**Base cooldown: 6s**

![tubularbells.gif](https://trello.com/1/cards/6ab91f8a8e1ff3d8c8ead3e0/attachments/6aba7e469cb9768da3be093f/previews/6aba7e479cb9768da3be0a94/download/tubularbells.gif)

## Spiked Balloon Swarm

**Inflate nearby metal into a swarm of small balloons, some covered in spikes. Bigger objects make larger swarms. They drift toward enemies ahead, stick to them, then burst. Each enemy can take up to five hits. (Cursor over object)**

**Base cooldown: 16s**

![carssssss.gif](https://trello.com/1/cards/6ab91f8a8e1ff3d8c8ead3e0/attachments/6aba810d32ee2cc0d317ff02/previews/6aba811432ee2cc0d3180417/download/carssssss.gif)

## EX [Awakening Moves]

## Balloon Beast [4 Bars]

**Combine one of each animal into a T-rex, or five of the same animal into a weaker giant. Pilot it, send it with Inflate or have it follow a scent. It swallows an enemy on contact and bursts inside them. Lasts up to 50 seconds and uses all five balloons.**

**Base cooldown: 40s**

## Babylonian Balloons [3 Bars]

**Turn your balloons into swords and fire them forward one after another. They stick into enemies, then burst and cause Bleed.**

**Base cooldown: 22s**

![babylonian.gif](https://trello.com/1/cards/6ab91f8a8e1ff3d8c8ead3e0/attachments/6aba8396d733fa4dd0480e5b/previews/6aba839dd733fa4dd04814a2/download/babylonian.gif)

## Balloon Parade [2 Bars]

**Fly your balloons together in a circling formation for up to 12 seconds. They latch onto enemies and burst. Use summon to drop their metal forms, or Pilot to return to your body.**

**Base cooldown: 20s**

![sss5.gif](https://trello.com/1/cards/6ab91f8a8e1ff3d8c8ead3e0/attachments/6aba84f98e62f15f07fd5fbb/previews/6aba850d8e62f15f07fd810e/download/sss5.gif)

## Lead Balloons [3 Bars]

**Send your balloons above an enemy, then pop them one after another. Their metal forms fall onto anyone underneath.**

**Base cooldown: 24s**
