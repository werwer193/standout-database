## Lore:

## “I’m becoming rich no matter what it takes!”

**The Stand of Jodio Joestar. November Rain produces rain beneath its body and lets Jodio control the weight of its drops. Its downpour can crush enemies, deflect bullets and break through concrete.**

## Hidden Piloting

**Use Ctrl while November Rain is unsummoned to move it quickly and leave it in position. You can use Make It Rain to summon it and start the downpour instantly. Taking a hit forces it to appear. If it is left beyond your range, it walks back to you.**

![image.webp](https://trello.com/1/cards/6af25326efcf475a0337d5cd/attachments/6af371b57324d769f776123a/previews/6af371b67324d769f77612a4/download/image.webp)

## Pressure

**Enemies gain one stack of Pressure per second in your rain. At one stack, attacks are slowed. At two stacks, movement slows too. At three, the enemy is crushed and the stacks clear. Outside the rain, they lose one stack per second. Multiple rain sources do not stack quicker.**

‌

## Make It Rain

**Toggle a downpour beneath November Rain.  Enemies caught in it take steady damage, become Soaked and build Pressure. The rain passes through roofs and floors. Press again to stop. While piloting the unsummoned Stand, this summons it at that spot and starts the rain.**

**Base cooldown: 8s**

## Rainfall

**Rain falls beneath November Rain, passing through roofs and floors for up to 60 studs below its feet. It applies Soaked without Cold, puts out fires and enlarges puddles it lands in.**

![raining.gif](https://trello.com/1/cards/6af25326efcf475a0337d5cd/attachments/6af3710e8f852e9ab5c43aed/previews/6af371108f852e9ab5c43df4/download/raining.gif)

## Tilt

**Only the stand tilts and fires a stream of rain; aiming does not turn the user’s body. Hold the skill to keep firing. The direction of the drops is aimable. Each drop briefly stuns, pushes enemies back, applies Soaked and builds Pressure. Rain that hits a flat ceiling leaves a puddle there for one minute. EX; Heavy Rain drops each puddle as a heavy drop onto whoever is below, crushing them.**

**Base cooldown: 12s**

![novemberrain.gif](https://trello.com/1/cards/6af25326efcf475a0337d5cd/attachments/6af3730b0409faac5e06adc0/previews/6af373150409faac5e06bd6d/download/novemberrain.gif)

## Skewer

**Stab the nearest enemy in reach with November Rain’s closest leg, koncking them down. Breaks guards, but can be perfect blocked. Deals 150% more damage while the Stand is enlarged. Deals bonus damage when the target is crushed by Pressure.**

**Base cooldown: 10s**

![skewer.gif](https://trello.com/1/cards/6af25326efcf475a0337d5cd/attachments/6af3736e7dd9ed6c2ae92d02/previews/6af373717dd9ed6c2ae9318e/download/skewer.gif)

## Isolation Veil

**Surround November Rain with a curtain of rain for 5 seconds. The rain stops all projectiles and forces thrown props straight down. While inside, you are also protected from two light melee attacks or one heavy hit before the curtain breaks. Higher ability extends the duration.**

**Base cooldown: 18s**

![veil.gif](https://trello.com/1/cards/6af25326efcf475a0337d5cd/attachments/6af3740f9619201636a3953b/previews/6af374129619201636a39b88/download/veil.gif)

## Spare

**Aim at a target within 150 studs to toggle protection from November Rain’s attacks. Use again on the same person to remove that protection. Your party members are always protected by default.**

**Base cooldown: 0.4s**

## EX Moves [Awakening Moves]

## Heavy Rain [2 Bars]

**Targets soaked from November Rain’s attacks are marked. Targets within 60 studs and visible to you or November Rain that are marked are slammed into the ground and Soaked status is removed from them. Blocking can prevent the slam. This skill also releases the water traps Tilt leaves on ceilings in order to crush targets.**

**Base cooldown: 24s**

![heavy.gif](https://trello.com/1/cards/6af25326efcf475a0337d5cd/attachments/6af37573bf0fa6ebd0f30dbd/previews/6af37576bf0fa6ebd0f314ed/download/heavy.gif)

## Higher Ground [1 Bar]

**Hold to hang beneath and steer November Rain as it carries you forward. Approach a wall to climb over it, or move/jump off a ledge to glide down. Release to let go or stop the walk. Taking a hit also cancels the skill. Cannot be used while piloting or while the Stand is enlarged.**

**Base cooldown: 10s**

## Who’ll Stop the Rain [1 Bar]

**Continually drain EX to extend November Rain’s legs to cover a much wider area with rain. The Stand moves much slower, but Skewer reaches farther and does increased damage and Isolation Veil grows with it. Higher ability reduces the drain. Press again to end the form.**

**Base cooldown: 6s**
