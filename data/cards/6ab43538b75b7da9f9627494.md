## Lore:

**“There’s a room inside the turtle?!”**

**The Stand of the turtle Coco Jumbo. It manifests as a special room located inside the gem of a key. When the key is placed in the turtle’s shell, it functions as a mobile safe house that can shelter humans.**

## BANKEY

**Activate the turtle’s key to change your fighting style.**

**Activate near your turtle** while it is **out of its shell** and you are **not piloting,** The Key changes your **fighting style** and enables its **special dash. Use the move again to turn it off.**

**Base cooldown: 45**s

## Key Dash

**Activate BANKEY**, then use **Q forward for the Key’s special dash**. **Side and backward dashes work as usual**. Shift lock is not required.

## Pilot Mode (LCTRL)

Summon your stand, Then use **Ctrl** to **enter or leave pilot control**. **Your camera and movement follow the stand.** **This stand stays on the ground while you pilot it.**
