# Lore:

## **”OSHAAAAAAA!”**

**Ermes Costello’s Stand creates stickers that duplicate whatever they touch. Removing a sticker forces the two copies back together, damaging them as they merge. She uses this power to duplicate limbs, turn objects into traps and pull herself toward distant objects.**

# Sticker

**Quickly place a sticker on an enemy or throwable. Hold to throw it. You can have six stickers out at once; placing another pops your oldest one. Spent stickers come back one every 4 seconds. Use Sticker Rip (G) to send Kizz to tear a sticker off and reunite its copies.**

**Tap to place a sticker within reach, including on a held object. Hold to throw one up to 120 studs. A successful touch or thrown sticker spends on sticker. You can place several stickers on the same original to make separate copies. Placement on enemies can be blocked or perfect blocked.**

**Use Sticker Rip (G) to send Kizz to the sticker and rip it off; it has a 1-second cooldown. Enemy stickers must be within 50 studs of you. Reuniting objects damage enemies in there path. Peeling an enemy’s limb copy hits them by limb: an arm deals 8 and slows their attacks for 4 seconds, a leg deals 7 and slows them for 4 seconds, and a head deals 13, stuns longest and blurs their vision for 3 seconds. Stickering a Burning Down the House user while they draw Ghost Revolver drags them along behind the round when it fires. Copies can be picked up, thrown, dropped and saved for later.**

# Kizz Olala

**Deliver a short kick barrage. The final kick knocks enemies back and leaves an arm sticker on anyone who does not already have one.**

**The kicks can be blocked; The final kick strains guards and can be perfect blocked. Follow up with G to peel the arm sticker. Double Trouble’s legs let you move faster during the barrage and extend its reach.**

# Yeet

**Kizz flies to the enemy or throwable nearest your cursor, lifts it overhead and heaves it the way your camera faces: one hard shove, then gravity does the rest. A Kizz copy of a prop flies much further.**

**Kizz locks onto the enemy nearest your cursor, or the throwable your cursor is on, within 22 studs, flies there, lifts it overhead and heaves it where your camera points. The grab can be blocked or perfect blocked, and armored, unstoppable or grab-immune enemies can’t be lifted. Thrown enemies take 8 damage and ragdoll for about a second, landing wherever the throw carries them.**

**Any throwable can be lifted this way. A Kizz copy of a prop flies three times as far, and a flying prop hurts anyone it hits. A body double is lifted like a heavy prop and flies as far as a thrown enemy, staying upright and spinning until it lands on its feet. With nothing in reach the skill doesn’t start; a miss puts it on a short cooldown.**

# Double Trouble

**Spend two stickers to duplicate your arms for 50% stronger light attacks, or your legs for 50% more speed and jump power. Tap with your cursor above the screen’s center for arms or below it for legs. Hold to apply this to someone else. Lasts 12 seconds.**

**Tap to duplicate your own limbs: aim above the middle of the screen for arms or below it for legs. Hold while aiming at someone nearby to duplicate their upper or lower limbs instead. Enemies can block or perfect block the application.**

**Applying Double Trouble spends two stickers. Arms strengthen light attacks by 50%. Legs increase movement speed and jump power by 50%, and improve Kizz Olala’s movement and reach. You can maintain one set at a time. A target cannot receive multiple sets.**

**The limbs merge after 12 seconds; pressing the skill again or dismissing Kizz doesn’t end them early. The person with the copied limbs loses 6 health when they merge, but cannot be knocked out by it. The 36-second cooldown starts when they merge; Sticker rip leaves them alone, and placing other stickers never pops them.**

# EX Moves [Awakening Moves]

## Body Double [3 EX BARS]

**Slap a sticker on an enemy and it rips straight off into a full body double of them that anyone can hit, knock around, carry or throw. Half of the damage the double takes is stored. Rip the sticker (G) to send the double crashing back into them and deal it all at once; rip it while carrying the double to drag them to you instead. The target has to protect their double.**

**Plane the sticker within reach; placement can be blocked or perfect blocked. The double is pushed out beside them at once. Anyone can hit it, knock it around, pick it up with H and throw it like a heavy prop; thrown, it flies as far as a thrown enemy, staying upright and spinning. It cannot be knocked out and does not fight back.**

**Half of the damage the double takes is banked. Use Sticker Rip (G) on the sticker: Kizz flies to the double, which crashes back into the original through anyone in the way and deals the banked damage all at once. Rip it while you carry the double to pull the original to you instead, then deal it, or carry it and use Return Trip to ride it straight into them. After 30 seconds the double goes back on its own no matter what, delivering the damage; the sticker also pops when either end is 300 studs from you or when newer stickers push it out, delivering it on the spot. The target’s defeat discards it; putting Kizz away does not. One double at a time. Smooth Operators' Rearrange pulls the sticker off with no damage.**

# Thread Recall [2 EX BARS]

**Kizz flies to the enemy nearest your cursor, latches on and swipes a copy of their clothing. Press again to yank them right up to you, or hold to toss the copy in the air and send it back into them.**

**Kizz flies to the enemy nearest your cursor within 32 studs and clings on for a moment while it copies their clothing; with nobody in range the skill doesn’t start. Keep the copy for up to 60 seconds while fighting. Tap again to yank the enemy right up to you, or hold to toss the copy up into the air; it then flies straight back into the them for 12 damage. A pull that cannot start leaves the copy in your hand.**

**The swipe, the pull and the thrown copy can be blocked or perfect blocked. Pulls and the thrown copy stop at walls. The copy is lost when time runs out, you dismiss Kizz, or either end moves more than 300 studs from you. Only the opening swipe costs EX; the cooldown starts when the copy is used or lost.**

# Return Trip [1 EX BAR]

**Grab a stickered object and ride it toward its other half. Kizz removes the sticker before you launch. Jump to let go early and keep your momentum.**

**Hold either half or stand within 14 studs or a loose half. Carrying an enemy’s body double, you ride straight into that enemy instead, and the double slams back into them when you arrive. You can ride toward the other half even while someone carries it, and picking it up does not stop the ride. You can move while Kizz approaches the sticker; press G to cancel before launch. Longer trips have more travel time, and walls stop your flight.**

**Jump to leave the ride early. The used copy disappears, while the original and its other stickers remain. Cannot be used while piloting.**

# MECHANICS

Sticker Collision

Aim at either half and tap G to send Kizz to its sticker. Kizz must reach it before peeling it. Enemy stickers must be within 50 studs of you. Hold G to have Kizz visit your stickers nearest first. Press it again during the approach to cancel. Your own limb stickers are left alone.

You can have six stickers out at once; placing another pops your oldest one. Spent stickers come back one at a time, every 4 seconds; one popped to make room is reused straight away. Keep up to twelve objects copies and sticker six enemies once. Object copies stay until merged, destroyed or you die or change Stand; distance and dismissing Kizz do not remove them. Enemy stickers last 60 seconds. Putting Kizz away never removes or hides a sticker.

# Pilot mode

Summon your stand, then use Ctrl to enter or leave pilot control. Your camera and movement follow the stand.

Hold T to use EX actions.
